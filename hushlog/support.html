Common questions

Where are my logs stored?

Only on your phone, in the app's private storage. There is no account and no cloud copy held by us. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include the app's data if you have backup turned on.

I logged at the wrong time. How do I fix it?

Tap Edit in the confirmation that appears after logging, or tap the entry on the timeline. Use the −15, −5, +5 and +15 buttons to move the time, then tap Save. You can also change the type, the bottle amount or add a note.

How does the nursing timer work?

Tap Left or Right to start. The side you did not use last is marked "next". Tap the other side to switch, or Finish feed to stop. Starting a sleep also ends a running feed, and starting a feed ends a running sleep.

What is night mode?

A dim, warm, amber-on-black palette that is easier on tired eyes in a dark room. By default it switches on automatically from 7 pm to 7 am. Use the moon button on Today or Settings → Night mode to change that.

Is the "next nap" window a prediction?

No. It shows a general awake-time range for your baby's age, of the kind found in common parenting guides. Every baby is different, and it is not medical advice.

How do I restore Plus on a new phone?

Open Settings → See Plus → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account. Your logs move only through your phone's own backup or transfer.

How do I cancel Plus?

On iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android, go to Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Logging stays free after you cancel.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete all data. To remove one baby and that baby's logs, open the baby in Settings and choose Remove.
