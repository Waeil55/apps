Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Hushlog app for iPhone, iPad and Android

The short version

  • Everything you log is stored only on your phone, and we never receive it. That includes feeds, sleep, diapers, notes, and your baby's name or nickname and birth date.
  • There is no Hushlog account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • There are no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking, and we never sell data.
  • If you subscribe to Plus, Apple or Google process the payment. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, sees an anonymous ID and your purchase status, and nothing about your baby.
  • You can delete everything at any time in Settings → Delete all data.

Who we are

Hushlog is made by Wael Rooma ("we", "us"). Contact: wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Who the app is for

Hushlog is a tool for adult parents and caregivers. It is not directed at children, and children are not meant to use it. A parent may type a baby's name or nickname and birth date. That information stays on the parent's device: it is used only to show the baby's age and to choose an age range for the general nap guide. We never receive it.

What stays on your device

Your logs, notes, baby profiles and settings are stored in a local database inside the app. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Your device's own backup system (iCloud or Google device backup) may include this data in your private backup, if you have backup turned on. That backup is controlled by your Apple or Google account, not by us.

If you create a caregiver summary PDF, it is generated on your device. It goes only where you choose to share it.

What is processed off the device

WhoWhatWhy
Apple App Store or Google PlayYour purchase and payment, under their own privacy policiesTo sell and renew a Plus subscription

We do not use RevenueCat's attribution or advertising integrations, and we do not link your purchase to your identity.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

We process subscription data because it is needed to perform our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not receive the logs on your device, so we are not their controller.

Retention and deletion

On-device data stays until you delete it. You can remove a single entry, remove a baby with all of that baby's logs, or use Settings → Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes it. RevenueCat keeps purchase records as long as needed for subscriptions, tax and accounting. To ask us to delete that record, email wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to processing. Because we hold almost nothing, most requests are handled by the delete controls in the app. For anything else, email us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Data is stored inside the app's private storage on your device and protected by your device's lock and encryption. Connections to the app stores and to RevenueCat use encryption in transit.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. If the change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
