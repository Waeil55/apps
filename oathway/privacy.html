Privacy policy

Version 1.0 is free. Oathway currently has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment or purchase data is processed and the purchase sections below do not apply yet. If paid features are added in a later version, this page will be updated before they launch.
Short version: your study progress stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We never see your answers.

Who we are

Oathway is published by Waeil Mikhaeil. Contact: wael.rooma@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

  • Your answers, review schedule, readiness score and mock interview history.
  • Your settings: study language, appearance and roughly when your interview is.

This data is stored locally in the app and is never uploaded to us or anyone else. Deleting the app, or Settings → Reset progress, removes it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat, Inc. to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, purchase receipts and basic device information (such as the operating system and app version), never your study data. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy at revenuecat.com. We never see your payment card details.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in, and no name, email, location, contacts, camera or microphone access.
  • No advertising, no advertising ID, and no selling or sharing of data for advertising.
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in this version.

Email

If you email support, we use your message and address only to answer you, and we delete them when they are no longer needed.

Children

Oathway is meant for adults preparing for the naturalization interview. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Your study data never leaves your device, so you control it directly. For questions about purchase data held by RevenueCat, Apple or Google, or to exercise rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, contact wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date.
