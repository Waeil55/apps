Oathway: citizenship test prep in English and Spanish

How does the readiness score work?

At the interview the officer asks up to 20 civics questions, and 12 correct answers pass. Oathway estimates how likely you are to answer each question correctly from how you have answered it over time, then works out your chance of getting 12 or more right. It is an estimate, not a guarantee.

Why do questions I missed come back?

A missed question returns about 10 minutes later, then after longer gaps (1, 3, 7, 16 and 35 days) as you keep getting it right. Questions that survive the one-week gap count as mastered.

Are these the official questions?

The practice questions are written from the public USCIS civics study materials, with the answers USCIS accepts. Multiple choice is for practice; at the interview the officer asks out loud. Check the official list at uscis.gov/citizenship, especially answers about current officials, which can change.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. There is no account, so deleting the app deletes your progress too.

Subscriptions and restoring purchases

Oathway Plus unlocks all 7 topics, unlimited mock interviews and detailed progress. On a new phone, open Settings → Restore purchases. To cancel, use your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
