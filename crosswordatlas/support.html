Common questions

How do I switch between Across and Down?

Tap the selected square again, or tap the clue bar above the keyboard. On a hardware keyboard, press the space bar or use the arrow keys.

What’s free?

Today’s Daily Mini, this week’s Weekly, practice Minis and the first puzzle in every themed pack, with check, reveal and streaks. Plus adds the archive, Midi and Large practice, and every themed pack.

How do streaks work?

Solve the Daily Mini on its own day to add one to your streak. Your streak stays until the end of the following day. Archive puzzles don’t change your streak.

Does using Reveal break my streak?

No. The solve still counts, but it won’t set a best time.

Can I play in Spanish?

Yes. Go to Settings → Puzzle and app language. Spanish puzzles follow the crossword convention of no accents, and the keyboard adds Ñ.

How do I restore Plus on a new phone?

Use the same Apple ID or Google account, open Settings in the app and tap “Restore purchases”.

How do I cancel Plus?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel.

I think a clue is wrong.

Thank you! Email us the date or pack, the clue number and what you expected. We fix clue mistakes in the next update.
