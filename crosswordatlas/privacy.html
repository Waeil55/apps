Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Crossword Atlas app for iPhone, iPad and Android

The short version

  • Your puzzles, progress, streaks and settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them.
  • There is no account. We don’t ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No ads, no advertising IDs, no analytics and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • The only data that leaves your device is what’s needed to check a Plus subscription, handled by RevenueCat.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.

Who we are

Crossword Atlas is provided by Wael Rooma (“we”, “us”). Contact us at wael.rooma@gmail.com with any privacy question.

Data that stays on your device

Puzzles are created on your device. The app stores the following locally, inside the app:

  • Letters you’ve entered, timers and which puzzles you’ve solved
  • Your streak, best times and solve counts
  • Your settings (language, appearance, timer and haptics)

We have no access to this data. If your device backup (iCloud or Google) is on, it may include the app’s data, under your Apple or Google account.

Sharing a result

If you tap “Share result”, the app opens your device’s share sheet with a short text such as your solve time. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

Data processed to manage your subscription

If you start a trial or subscribe to Crossword Atlas Plus, payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card details.

To check whether your subscription is active and to restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. as our service provider. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created on your device (not your name or email)
  • your purchase records from Apple or Google (product, dates, status, country, price)
  • basic technical data such as app version, operating system version and IP address, needed to deliver the service

RevenueCat processes this data only on our behalf, under its own privacy policy and data processing terms.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising SDKs, advertising IDs or tracking pixels.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services in this version.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data, including for targeted advertising.
  • No use of your data to train AI models.

Legal bases (UK and EU users)

Subscription data is processed to perform our contract with you (providing Crossword Atlas Plus) and to meet legal obligations such as tax records.

Your rights and choices

  • Deletion: uninstalling the app removes all progress from your device.
  • Subscription data: email us to request access to or deletion of the subscription records held by RevenueCat. We may need to keep some purchase records where the law requires.
  • Complaints: in the UK or EU you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Retention

Data on your device stays until you delete the app. Subscription records are kept by RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal and tax obligations.

International transfers

RevenueCat is based in the United States. Where subscription data is transferred from the UK or EU, it is protected by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Children

Crossword Atlas suits all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Free puzzles work without any data leaving the device, and purchases need the store account holder’s approval.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.
