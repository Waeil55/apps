Table Talk

Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

The short version: Table Talk has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The player names you type and the record of which cards you've played are stored only on your device. We, Wael Rooma, can't see them.

Who we are

Table Talk ("the app") is made by Wael Rooma. You can reach us at wael.rooma@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

  • Player names you add, and their seating order.
  • Your settings: appearance, language, haptics, and whether grown-up packs are shown.
  • Your game setup choices, which cards your group has already played, the number of games played, and your best In Sync score.

This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us. To delete it, go to Settings → Reset all data, or uninstall the app.

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it doesn't use your advertising identifier.

Purchases

If you subscribe to Table Talk Plus, the payment is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat, Inc. to confirm which purchases are active. RevenueCat receives the store's purchase receipt and an anonymous, randomly generated app user id. It doesn't receive your name, email, player names or anything you do in the game. See RevenueCat's privacy policy, plus the privacy policies of Apple and Google.

Children

Table Talk is a general-audience party game meant to be played with adults present. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age where you live), because the app collects no personal information at all.

Your rights

Because we don't hold any of your personal data, there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your data lives on your device and you control it. Questions about purchase records should go to Apple or Google, or to us at the address above, and we'll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, where the change is significant, mention it in the app's release notes.
