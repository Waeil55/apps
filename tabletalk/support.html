How many people can play?

From 2 up to 12. Odd One Out needs at least 3 players. Act It Out uses two teams once you have 4 or more players, or you can switch teams off.

Do we need the internet?

No. Every game works offline. The internet is only used when you buy or restore Table Talk Plus.

How do I restore my subscription on a new phone?

Open Settings → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

Where are the grown-up packs?

They're hidden by default. Go to Settings → Grown-up packs and confirm that everyone at the table is 18 or older. They're clean, with no explicit content.

We keep seeing the same cards.

Table Talk deals cards your group hasn't played yet first. Once a pack runs out, it starts over. Mixing several packs, or adding Plus, gives you more variety.

¿Está en español?

Sí. Table Talk sigue el idioma de tu teléfono, o puedes elegirlo en Ajustes → Idioma.
