Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 is free. Mindspan currently has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment or purchase data is processed and the purchase sections below do not apply yet. If paid features are added in a later version, this page will be updated before they launch.

Effective 13 November 2026 · Wael Rooma

  • Your scores, streaks and settings stay on your device.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google, with RevenueCat checking that a purchase is valid.

What stays on your device

Mindspan creates each day's session on your phone from the date. Your game scores, accuracy, play history, streaks and settings (appearance and haptics) are stored only in the app's local storage. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

If you subscribe to Mindspan Plus, the App Store or Google Play processes your payment. We never see your card details. The app uses RevenueCat, a service provider, to confirm that your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID and your purchase history from the store. It does not receive your name, email or game data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads or use an advertising identifier.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in this version.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.
  • We do not collect health information. Mindspan is a game, not a medical or diagnostic product.

Sharing your results

When you tap Share, the app hands a short text (the date, each game's score, your total and your streak) to your device's share sheet. Where it goes next is your choice. It never includes personal data.

Children

Mindspan is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can delete all app data at any time by deleting the app. Manage or cancel a subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Questions: wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, explain them in the app's release notes.
