Common questions

When does a new session arrive?

At midnight in your local time zone. Everyone gets the same three games on the same day.

How do streaks work?

Finish all three games of the daily session on the same day to add a day to your streak. Your streak stays safe until midnight.

Can I replay a session game?

Yes. Only your first run counts toward the day's total; later runs are saved as free play.

How is the score worked out?

Quick-answer games give 10 points per correct answer, plus a small bonus for answers in a row. Echo Grid gives 10 points per tile in each round you clear. Glimpse gives 15 points per exact count.

Does Mindspan improve memory or health?

Mindspan is a set of games for fun and practice. We make no medical or health claims.

I bought Plus on another device

Open Settings (the sliders icon on Today), then Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account.

How do I cancel?

In your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Plus stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. If you delete the app or change phones, your history does not move with you in this version.
