Common questions

How do I play?

Tap a jar to lift it, then tap another jar to pour. Pigment pours onto the same colour or into an empty jar, as much as fits. A puzzle is done when every colour sits in a jar of its own. Solve a level in par moves or fewer for three stars.

What are veils, odd glass and the dish?

Veiled layers are grey until they reach the top of their jar, then they show their colour. In Odd Glass levels, jars come in three sizes and each colour fits exactly one of them. In The Dish, you get one spare jar and a small dish that holds two layers.

I am stuck. What can I do?

Undo and Hint are free and unlimited. Hint lifts a jar and marks where to pour it. Restart puts the level back to its start. Every level has been checked to be solvable.

What is the Daily Pour?

The Daily Pour is a new puzzle each day, the same for everyone. Solving it on consecutive days builds your streak. It is free forever.

I bought Spillsort Complete on another device. How do I get it back?

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account, then go to Settings → Restore purchase, or tap Restore on the purchase screen. A purchase made through Apple doesn't carry over to Google, or the other way round.

How do I get a refund?

Apple and Google handle all refunds. For Apple, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google, go to play.google.com, open Order history and request a refund.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Your progress is stored only on your device, because there is no account. A full device backup and restore through iCloud or Google usually brings it across. You can always restore the purchase, even if your progress doesn't come across.

Accessibility options

Settings → Pattern marks puts a distinct shape on every colour for colour-blind play. Reduce motion also follows your system setting, and sound and haptics can be switched off. Text follows your system text size, and every jar is labelled for VoiceOver and TalkBack with its colours from the top down.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Reset progress erases everything Spillsort has stored on your device, and deleting the app also removes it. We keep no gameplay data on any server. See the Privacy Policy.
