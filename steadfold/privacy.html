Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 is free. Steadfold currently has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment or purchase data is processed and the purchase sections below do not apply yet. If paid features are added in a later version, this page will be updated before they launch.

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Steadfold app for iPhone and Android

The short version

  • Everything you log (doses, side effects, meals, water, weight, workouts, notes) is stored only on your phone. We never receive it.
  • There is no Steadfold account. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number to use the app.
  • No ads, no advertising IDs, no tracking across apps or websites, and no analytics on your health logs.
  • We do not sell or share your data, and we never will.
  • You can export everything or delete everything from Settings at any time.

Who we are

Steadfold is provided by Wael Rooma ("we", "us"). Contact us at wael.rooma@gmail.com with any privacy question.

Data that stays on your phone

Steadfold is built to work without a server. The following data is created by you and stored in a private database inside the app on your device:

  • Medication details you type in (the name you choose, schedule, and the dose exactly as written on your label)
  • Dose log entries, injection sites and notes
  • Side-effect entries, severity and notes
  • Protein, water, meal presets, weight and measurements
  • Workouts, habit check-ins, questions for your doctor
  • Your settings, units and reminder times

We have no access to this data. Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone, so they don't pass through any server either.

Device backups. If you have iCloud Backup or Google device backup turned on, your phone may include the app's data in that backup. That backup is controlled by Apple or Google under your account, not by us.

Sharing you choose. If you create a visit summary PDF or an export file, it is saved or shared only where you choose (for example, email to your doctor). After that, it is handled by whoever you shared it with.

Data processed to manage your subscription

If you start a trial or subscribe to Steadfold Plus, payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card details.

To check whether your subscription is active and to restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. as our service provider. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created on your device (not your name or email)
  • your purchase and subscription records from Apple or Google (product, dates, status, country, price)
  • basic technical data such as app version, operating system version and IP address, needed to deliver the service

RevenueCat does not receive any of your health logs. It processes this data only on our behalf, under its own privacy policy and data processing terms.

Crash reports

This version of Steadfold does not send crash reports. If we turn crash reporting on in a future version, we will update this policy first, and reports will contain only technical diagnostics, never your health logs.

What we don't do

  • We don't use advertising SDKs, advertising IDs or tracking pixels.
  • We don't send your health logs to analytics or any third party.
  • We don't sell, rent or share personal data, including for targeted advertising.
  • We don't use your data to train AI models.

Consumer health data

Some US state laws (including Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada's SB 370) give extra rights over "consumer health data". The health information you enter in Steadfold stays on your device; we do not collect, share or sell it. If that ever changes, we will ask for your separate consent first and update this policy.

Legal bases (UK and EU users)

Your health logs are processed on your device under your explicit consent, which you give during setup and can withdraw at any time by deleting your data. Subscription data is processed to perform our contract with you (providing Steadfold Plus) and to meet legal obligations such as tax records.

Your rights and choices

  • Access and portability: Settings → Your data → Export gives you all your data as JSON and CSV files.
  • Deletion: Settings → Your data → Delete all data erases everything in the app. Deleting the app also removes it from your phone.
  • Subscription data: email us to request access to or deletion of the subscription records held by RevenueCat. We may need to keep some purchase records where the law requires.
  • Complaints: if you are in the UK or EU, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not discriminate against anyone for exercising their privacy rights.

Retention

Data on your phone stays until you delete it. Subscription records are kept by RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal and tax obligations.

Security

Your data is protected by your phone's own security (passcode, biometrics and device encryption). Because we don't hold your health logs on a server, there is no central database of them to breach.

International transfers

RevenueCat is based in the United States. Where subscription data is transferred from the UK or EU, it is protected by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Children

Steadfold is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly process data about children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.

Steadfold is a logging and reminder tool. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Always follow your prescriber's instructions.
