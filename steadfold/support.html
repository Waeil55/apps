Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone, inside the app. There's no account and we never receive your logs. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Your phone's own backup (iCloud Backup or Google backup) usually restores app data when you set up a new phone. You can also use Settings → Your data → Export all data (JSON) or Export spreadsheets (CSV) to keep a copy.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Your data → Delete all data. This can't be undone, so export first if you want a copy.

Why does the app show my dose exactly as I typed it?

Steadfold records your dose as written on your pharmacy or prescription label. It never calculates, converts or suggests doses. If your dose changes, your prescriber will tell you; update it in Settings → Medication and dose.

My reminders aren't arriving

Check that notifications are allowed for Steadfold in your phone's settings, that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing them, and (on Android) that battery optimisation isn't stopping the app. Then open Settings → Reminders in Steadfold and save your times again.

How do I restore my Steadfold Plus purchase?

Open Settings → Subscription → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Steadfold. On Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Steadfold. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple or Google, so refunds are handled by them: reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, or the Google Play order history for Android.

Does Steadfold connect to Apple Health or Health Connect?

Not yet. It's planned for a future version.
