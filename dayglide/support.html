Common questions

How do I add a block?

On Today, tap “Add a block”, give it a name, then pick a time and length. Tap a dashed “Free time” row to add a block in that gap.

How do I start the timer?

Tap any block to open Focus, then tap “Start timer”. A block that is happening right now counts down on its own.

How do repeating routines work?

Open a routine and choose the days under “Repeat on”. On those days it’s added to your timeline automatically. If you remove it from a day, it won’t come back that day.

I’m not getting reminders

Check Settings → Reminders in Dayglide, then make sure notifications for Dayglide are allowed in your phone’s settings. Focus modes and battery savers can also delay notifications.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, then go to Settings → Restore purchases. Your plan itself stays on the old device unless you exported it or your device backup includes it.

How do I cancel Plus?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Dayglide. On Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Dayglide. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Your data → Delete everything. We hold no copy of your plan, so this deletes it completely.
