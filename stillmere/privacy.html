Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 is free. Stillmere currently has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment or purchase data is processed and the purchase sections below do not apply yet. If paid features are added in a later version, this page will be updated before they launch.

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Stillmere app for iPhone, iPad and Android

The short version

  • Your focus sessions, the notes you give them ("what are you focusing on?"), your valleys and your settings are stored only on your phone. We never receive them.
  • There is no Stillmere account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Break reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. No push server is involved.
  • There are no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking, and we never sell data.
  • If you subscribe to Plus, Apple or Google process the payment. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, sees an anonymous ID and your purchase status, and nothing about your sessions.
  • You can delete everything at any time in Settings → Delete my data.

Who we are

Stillmere is made by Wael Rooma ("we", "us"). Contact: wael.rooma@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your finished focus sessions (when they started and ended, how long they were, and the optional note you typed), the state of a running timer, and your settings are stored in a local database inside the app. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Your device's own backup system (iCloud or Google device backup) may include this data in your private backup, if you have backup turned on. That backup is controlled by your Apple or Google account, not by us.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Stillmere schedules them on your device for the moment a focus session or a break ends. They are created and delivered by your phone's operating system. We do not use a push notification service and do not receive a device token.

What is processed off the device

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

We process subscription data because it is needed to perform our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not receive the data on your device, so we are not its controller.

Children

Stillmere is a general productivity app for teenagers and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Retention and deletion

On-device data stays until you delete it. You can delete a single session in Stats, or everything in Settings → Delete my data. Deleting the app also removes it. RevenueCat keeps purchase records as long as needed for subscriptions, tax and accounting. To ask us to delete that record, email wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to processing. Because we hold almost nothing, most requests are handled by the delete controls in the app. For anything else, email us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Data is stored inside the app's private storage on your device and protected by your device's lock and encryption. Connections to the app stores and to RevenueCat use encryption in transit.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. If the change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
