Common questions

How does the valley grow?

Each focus session you finish plants one more thing in today's valley: trees, flowers, a cottage after four sessions, a lake after eight. While a session runs, you can watch the next one grow in. Each day starts a new valley. Older ones are in Stats → Your valleys.

What happens if I stop a session early?

Nothing is lost. Tap Reset and the session simply isn't recorded, so it doesn't plant anything. There's no penalty and nothing withers.

Does the timer keep running if I leave the app?

Yes. Stillmere keeps time by the clock, so the timer stays right when your phone sleeps or the app is closed. When you come back, finished sessions are recorded.

I don't get break reminders

Check that Settings → Break reminders is on in Stillmere, and that notifications for Stillmere are allowed in your phone's settings. Focus modes and battery saver can delay or hide notifications.

How do I change the session lengths?

Settings → Timer. Focus can be 15 to 45 minutes (50 to 90 with Plus), short breaks 3 to 10 minutes, long breaks 10 to 30 minutes, and you can choose after how many sessions the long break comes.

How do I cancel Plus?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Stillmere. On Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Stillmere. Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

I bought Plus but the app shows Free

Open Settings → See Plus → Restore, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

How do I move my sessions to a new phone?

Your data lives in the app on your device. A full device backup and restore (iCloud or Google) normally brings it along. There is no Stillmere account or cloud sync.

How do I delete my data?

Delete one session in Stats → Today's sessions, or everything in Settings → Delete my data. Uninstalling the app also removes it.
