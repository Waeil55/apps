Common questions

How do I log my period?

On Today, tap "Period started today". To mark other days, open Calendar, tap "Edit period days", tap each day of your period, then tap Done. Tap any past day to add flow, symptoms, mood or a note.

How are the estimates worked out?

Tidewell averages the length of your recent cycles (up to six) and periods. Until you have logged two periods, it uses the usual lengths you set in Settings. The fertile window is estimated around 14 days before your next estimated period. Cycles that look like a missed log (shorter than 15 days or longer than 60) are shown but left out of the averages.

Can I use Tidewell to avoid or plan a pregnancy?

No. Tidewell is not a method of contraception or a fertility treatment, and its dates are only estimates. Talk to a doctor or nurse about the options that are right for you.

My period is late. What does Tidewell do?

Today shows how many days later than estimated it is, and the calendar shows the next estimate from tomorrow. When your period starts, log it and every estimate updates. Cycles vary for many reasons; if you are worried, speak to a health professional.

Where is my data stored? Can I move it to a new phone?

Only on your phone. There is no account and no Tidewell cloud. On iPhone, a device-to-device transfer or your own encrypted iCloud Backup can carry it to a new iPhone. On Android, Tidewell is excluded from Google cloud backup for privacy, so export your logs (Plus) before you switch phones.

I forgot my passcode.

Because there is no account, a passcode can't be recovered or reset by us. On the lock screen, tap "Forgot passcode?" to erase all data and start again.

Why does the reminder not mention my period?

For privacy. Notifications can appear on your lock screen, so the reminder only says that a check-in is ready.

How do I restore Plus on a new phone?

Open Settings → See Plus → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account.

How do I cancel Plus?

On iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android, go to Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Your logs stay, and the free features keep working.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete all data. It erases every log and setting on the phone and can't be undone.
