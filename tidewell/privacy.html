Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Tidewell app for iPhone, iPad and Android

The short version

  • Your cycle logs are stored only on your phone, and we never receive them. That includes period dates, flow, symptoms, moods, notes, estimates and settings.
  • There is no Tidewell account. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or date of birth.
  • There are no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking. We never sell or share health information, and we could not if asked, because we do not have it.
  • If you subscribe to Plus, Apple or Google process the payment. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, sees an anonymous ID and your purchase status, and nothing about your cycle.
  • You can delete everything at any time in Settings → Delete all data.

Who we are

Tidewell is made by Wael Rooma ("we", "us"). Contact: wael.rooma@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Everything you log in Tidewell is kept in a local database inside the app's private storage: the days you mark as period or spotting, flow, symptoms, moods, notes, your usual cycle and period length, your appearance and reminder choices, and a salted hash of your passcode if you set one (never the passcode itself). Tidewell works fully offline and does not send any of this to us or to anyone else.

Backups. On Android, Tidewell opts out of Google cloud backup, so your logs are not copied to Google Drive. On iPhone and iPad, your device's own iCloud Backup may include app data in your private, encrypted backup if you have it turned on. That backup is controlled by your Apple account, not by us.

Exports. If you export a CSV file (Plus), it is created on your device and goes only where you choose to send it, for example to your own files or to your doctor. Once it leaves the app, it is outside Tidewell's protection.

Reminders. If you turn on the period reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. Its text never mentions your cycle, and nothing is sent to a server to deliver it.

What is processed off the device

Requests from others

We do not hold your cycle data, so we cannot hand it to anyone, including in response to a legal demand. Only you, with your unlocked phone, can see it.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

We process subscription data because it is needed to perform our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not receive the health data on your device, so we are not its controller and do not process special category data.

Children

Tidewell is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Retention and deletion

On-device data stays until you delete it. You can clear a single day, or use Settings → Delete all data to erase every log and setting at once. Deleting the app also removes it. If you forget your passcode, the only way back in is to erase all data, because there is no account to recover it with. RevenueCat keeps purchase records as long as needed for subscriptions, tax and accounting. To ask us to delete that record, email wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, the UK GDPR, or US state laws such as the CCPA and Washington's My Health My Data Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to processing. Because we hold almost nothing, most requests are handled by the delete controls in the app. For anything else, email us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Your logs live inside the app's private storage and are protected by your phone's own encryption and lock screen. The optional Tidewell passcode adds a second lock so someone holding your unlocked phone cannot open the app. Connections to the app stores and to RevenueCat use encryption in transit.

Tidewell is for personal tracking only. It is not a medical device and is not a method of contraception.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. If the change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
