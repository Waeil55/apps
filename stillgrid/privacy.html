Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 is free. Stillgrid currently has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment or purchase data is processed and the purchase sections below do not apply yet. If paid features are added in a later version, this page will be updated before they launch.

Effective 13 November 2026

In short

  • Stillgrid has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your puzzles, times, streaks and settings are stored only on your device.
  • If you buy Premium, Apple or Google handles payment. Our purchase service, RevenueCat, sees an anonymous ID and your purchase receipt, never your name or card.
  • We do not sell or share personal data, and we never will.

Who we are

Stillgrid is published by Waeil Mikhaeil ("we", "us"). Questions about privacy: wael.rooma@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

The puzzle in progress, your notes, solve times, statistics, daily streak and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your phone or tablet. They are not uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes them. Daily puzzles are generated on the device from the date, so no network connection is needed to play.

Purchases

Stillgrid Premium is an optional one-time purchase made through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google processes the payment under their own privacy policies; we never receive your payment details.

To confirm and restore purchases we use RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID created on your device, the purchase receipt from the store, your device type, operating system version and app version, and your approximate country (from the store's price region). It does not receive your name, email or contacts. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we do not collect

No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. No advertising SDKs or advertising ID. No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access. No sign-in.

Children

Stillgrid is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can erase your statistics at any time in Settings, or delete the app to remove everything. For purchase records held by RevenueCat, email us and we will ask RevenueCat to delete the data linked to your anonymous ID. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If a change affects what data leaves your device, the app will tell you before it happens.
