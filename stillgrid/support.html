Help and support

Stillgrid is a quiet daily sudoku. Write to wael.rooma@gmail.com and a real person will reply, usually within two working days.

How to play

Fill every row, column and 3×3 box with the digits 1 to 9, each once. Tap a square, then tap a number. Turn on Notes to pencil in possibilities. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, so it can always be finished by logic.

Common questions

When does the daily puzzle change?

At midnight on your device's clock. Everyone gets the same puzzle for the same date. The week builds gently: Easy on Monday and Tuesday, Medium on Wednesday and Thursday, Hard on Friday and Saturday, and Expert on Sunday.

How do streaks work?

Solve the daily puzzle on consecutive days to grow your streak. It stays alive until the end of the day after your last solve, so solving tomorrow keeps it going.

What do hints do?

A hint fixes the selected square if it is empty or wrong. Otherwise it fills the square that is easiest to work out next. Free play includes three hints per puzzle; Premium has no limit. Hints can't be undone.

Can I turn off red mistakes or the timer?

Yes. In Settings you can turn off "Show mistakes", "Show timer", matching-digit highlights and automatic note tidying.

How do I restore Premium on a new phone?

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account, open Settings in Stillgrid and tap Restore purchase. Premium is a one-time purchase, so you never pay twice.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases go through Apple or Google, so refunds are requested from them: on iPhone at reportaproblem.apple.com, on Android in your Google Play order history.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Your stats and streak are stored only on your device, for privacy. Device backups (iCloud or Google) may carry them over, but we can't promise it.

Accessibility

Stillgrid supports VoiceOver and TalkBack (every square is read with its row, column, value and notes), large text, dark mode and Reduce Motion. If something gets in your way, please tell us.
