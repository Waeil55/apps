Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Vigil app for iPhone, iPad and Android

The short version

  • Your fast plans, fast times and journal entries are stored only on your phone. We never receive them.
  • There is no Vigil account, no analytics SDK, no advertising and no tracking.
  • If you subscribe, Apple or Google handles the payment. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives an anonymous id and your purchase receipt, nothing about your prayers, fasts or journal.

Who we are

Vigil is published by Wael Rooma ("we", "us"). Contact: wael.rooma@gmail.com.

What Vigil keeps on your device

DataWhyWhere it goes
The fast plan you started, its start date and the days you marked as prayedTo show the right day's Scripture and your progress.A small database inside the app on your device.
Fast timer start and end times, and the length you choseTo run the timer and show your recent fasts.The same on-device database.
Journal entries you writeSo you can read them again.The same on-device database.
Settings (appearance, last timer length)To remember your preferences.The same on-device database.

Journal entries and fasting times can reveal religious beliefs, which some laws treat as sensitive. That is one reason Vigil keeps them on your device and never sends them to us.

Subscriptions

Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to check whether you have an active Vigil Plus subscription. RevenueCat receives a random app user id, your store receipt, the app version and your country and currency. It does not receive your plans, fasts, journal or settings. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no email collection, no contacts, no location, no camera, no photo library access.
  • No advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics, no data brokers, no selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No health data: Vigil does not read or write Apple Health or Health Connect, and it records no weight, food or body measurements.

Backups

The app's database may be included in your phone's own encrypted device backup (iCloud or Google). That backup is controlled by you and your device provider.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your Vigil data: Settings → Delete my data. This removes every plan, fast, journal entry and setting from the device.
  • Uninstall: removes everything Vigil stored.

Because we hold no personal data about you on our servers, access or deletion requests to us usually have nothing to return. For subscription data held by Apple, Google or RevenueCat, we will help you contact them. Residents of the EEA, UK and California have rights of access, deletion and objection; write to wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Children

Vigil is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app before they apply.
