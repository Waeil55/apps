Questions

How do I start a fast plan?

On Today, tap Choose a fast, pick a plan and tap Start today (or Start tomorrow). Each day shows a passage of Scripture, a prayer prompt and a reflection. Tap Mark day prayed when you have spent time with it.

What is free, and what is Vigil Plus?

Free: the one-day fast and the 7-day partial fast with daily Scripture and prayer prompts, the fast timer with history, the journal, and the day 1 reflection of every plan. Vigil Plus adds the Daniel fast (10 and 21 days), the 21-day fast and a reflection for every day.

Is Vigil safe for my health?

Vigil is a prayer companion and gives no medical advice. Please talk to a doctor before fasting from food if you are pregnant or nursing, have diabetes or another condition, take medication, are under 18 or have a history of disordered eating. A fast from media or a single food is a real fast too. Drink water and stop if you feel unwell.

How does the timer work?

Choose a length on the Timer tab and tap Start. The timer keeps running if you close the app, because it saves the start time. End it whenever you need to; every fast is saved with the time you kept.

Where is my journal stored? Can I move it to a new phone?

Only on your phone. It is included in your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) if you use one, and comes back when you restore that backup to a new phone.

Restore a purchase or cancel

Open the Plus screen from Settings and tap Restore. To cancel, use your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

Which Bible translation does Vigil use?

The World English Bible (WEB), a modern English translation in the public domain.

Delete my data

Settings → Delete my data removes every plan, fast and journal entry from your device. Uninstalling does the same.
