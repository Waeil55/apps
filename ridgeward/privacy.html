Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 is free. Ridgeward currently has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment or purchase data is processed and the purchase sections below do not apply yet. If paid features are added in a later version, this page will be updated before they launch.

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Ridgeward: Pocket Tactics game for iPhone, iPad and Android

The short version

  • Ridgeward collects no personal data. Your stats, settings and progress stay only on your device.
  • There is no account, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • There are no ads, no advertising IDs, no analytics and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • The only network use is checking and restoring the optional one-time purchase.
  • Settings → Reset progress deletes everything the game has stored.

Who we are

Ridgeward is provided by Waeil Mikhaeil ("we", "us"). Contact us at wael.rooma@gmail.com with any privacy question.

Data that stays on your device

Ridgeward plays offline. The game stores the following data in its private storage on your device:

  • Your settings: sound, haptics, motion, danger zones and your chosen squad
  • Your progress: stars per mission, battles played, daily streak and Daily Skirmish results
  • A battle in progress, so you can pick up where you left off

We have no access to this data. It leaves your device only in these two ways:

  • Device backups. If you use iCloud Backup or Google device backup, your device may include the app's data in that backup. Apple or Google controls that backup under your account.
  • Sharing you choose. If you tap "Share result" after a Daily Skirmish, the game puts a short text summary (skirmish number, stars and turns used) on your device's share sheet or clipboard. You decide where it goes. The text contains no link or identifier.

The optional purchase

"Ridgeward Premium" is a one-time purchase. Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) handles the payment, and we never see your payment details.

To confirm the unlock and restore it on your other devices, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. as our service provider. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created on your device (not your name or email)
  • your Ridgeward purchase records from Apple or Google: product, date, status, country and price
  • basic technical data needed to deliver the service, such as app version, operating system version and IP address

RevenueCat processes this data only on our behalf, under its own privacy policy and data processing terms. It receives none of your gameplay data.

What we don't do

  • We don't use advertising SDKs, advertising IDs or tracking pixels.
  • This version doesn't use analytics or crash-reporting services. If we ever add crash reporting, we will update this policy first, and reports will contain only technical diagnostics.
  • We don't sell, rent or share personal data.
  • We don't use your data to train AI models.
  • The game does not use your microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos.

Children

Ridgeward is suitable for all ages, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children. The store's own parental controls govern purchases, such as Apple's Ask to Buy or Google Play purchase approvals.

Legal bases (UK and EU users)

We process purchase data through RevenueCat to perform our contract with you (providing the unlock you bought) and to meet legal obligations such as keeping tax records. We do not process your gameplay data: it stays on your device.

Your rights and choices

  • Deletion: Settings → Reset progress erases everything stored on your device. Deleting the app also removes it.
  • Purchase records: email us to ask for access to, or deletion of, the records held by RevenueCat. We may need to keep some purchase records where the law requires.
  • Complaints: if you are in the UK or EU, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not treat anyone differently for using their privacy rights.

Retention

Data on your device stays until you reset it or delete the app. RevenueCat keeps purchase records as long as it needs them to provide the service and meet legal and tax obligations.

International transfers

RevenueCat is based in the United States. When purchase data is transferred from the UK or EU, appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses protect it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. For important changes, we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
