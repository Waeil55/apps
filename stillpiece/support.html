How to play

How do I place a piece?

Drag a piece up from the tray at the bottom onto the picture. When it is close to its spot, it snaps in and locks. Prefer not to drag? Tap a piece to select it, then tap the place on the board where it belongs.

How do I make a puzzle easier or harder?

When you open a picture, choose 12 (Relaxed), 30 (Classic), 48 (Detailed) or 80 (Expert) pieces. You can play any picture at any size, as often as you like.

What do the eye, Edges and Hint buttons do?

The eye shows or hides a faint guide picture on the board. Edges filters the tray to border pieces so you can build the frame first. Hint highlights where a piece goes and moves it to the front of the tray.

How does the Daily puzzle and streak work?

There is a new picture every day, free for everyone. Finish it on consecutive days to grow your streak. If you miss a day, the streak simply starts again; nothing is lost.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically after every piece and whenever you leave a puzzle. Up to twelve puzzles in progress are kept. Progress lives only on your device.

Purchases

What does Stillpiece Premium include?

Seven more picture packs (Highlands, Meadows, Coastlines, Nightfall, Botanica, Mosaic and Glasswork), 56 artworks in all. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. The Daily puzzle, all piece counts and the Daybreak pack are free forever.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account, open Stillpiece, then go to Settings → Restore purchase (it is also on the unlock screen).

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store. On iPhone and iPad use reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android, request one from your Google Play order history.

About the artwork

Every picture is an original illustration generated by Stillpiece's own code and dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. There are no ads and no tracking in the app.
