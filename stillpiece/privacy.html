Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 is free. Stillpiece currently has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment or purchase data is processed and the purchase sections below do not apply yet. If paid features are added in a later version, this page will be updated before they launch.

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Stillpiece app for iPhone, iPad and Android

The short version

  • Stillpiece collects no personal data. Your puzzles, records and settings stay on your device.
  • There is no account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • There are no ads, no advertising IDs, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The only network use is checking and restoring the optional one-time purchase.
  • Settings → Reset progress deletes everything the app has stored.

Who we are

Stillpiece is provided by Waeil Mikhaeil ("we", "us"). Contact us at wael.rooma@gmail.com with any privacy question.

Data that stays on your device

Stillpiece works offline. It stores the following in its private storage on your device:

  • Your settings: guide picture, haptics, reduced motion and your last piece count
  • Your records: puzzles solved, best times and your Daily puzzle streak
  • Puzzles in progress, so you can pick up where you left off

We have no access to this data. It leaves your device only through device backups (iCloud Backup or Google device backup), which Apple or Google controls under your account.

The optional purchase

"Stillpiece Premium" is a one-time purchase. Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) handles the payment, and we never see your payment details.

To confirm the unlock and restore it on your other devices, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. as our service provider. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created on your device (not your name or email)
  • your Stillpiece purchase records from Apple or Google: product, date, status, country and price
  • basic technical data needed to deliver the service, such as app version, operating system version and IP address

RevenueCat processes this data only on our behalf, under its own privacy policy and data processing terms. It receives none of your puzzle data.

What we don't do

  • We don't use advertising SDKs, advertising IDs or tracking pixels.
  • This version doesn't use analytics or crash-reporting services. If we ever add crash reporting, we will update this policy first.
  • We don't sell, rent or share personal data, and we don't use it to train AI models.
  • The app does not use your camera, microphone, photos, location or contacts.

Children

Stillpiece suits all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases are governed by the store's own parental controls, such as Apple's Ask to Buy or Google Play purchase approvals.

Legal bases (UK and EU users)

We process purchase data through RevenueCat to perform our contract with you (providing the unlock you bought) and to meet legal obligations such as keeping tax records. Your puzzle data stays on your device and we do not process it.

Your rights and choices

  • Deletion: Settings → Reset progress erases everything stored on your device. Deleting the app also removes it.
  • Purchase records: email us to ask for access to, or deletion of, the records held by RevenueCat. We may need to keep some records where the law requires.
  • Complaints: in the UK or EU you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Retention and transfers

Data on your device stays until you reset it or delete the app. RevenueCat keeps purchase records as long as it needs them for the service and for legal and tax obligations. RevenueCat is based in the United States; transfers from the UK or EU are protected by safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. For important changes, we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
