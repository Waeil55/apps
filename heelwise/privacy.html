Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 is free. Heelwise currently has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment or purchase data is processed and the purchase sections below do not apply yet. If paid features are added in a later version, this page will be updated before they launch.

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Heelwise app for iPhone and Android

The short version

  • Everything you log about your dog is stored only on your phone: training sessions, potty logs, behaviour notes and questions. We never receive it.
  • There is no Heelwise account. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number to use the app.
  • There are no ads or advertising IDs. Nothing tracks you across apps or websites, and nothing analyses your logs.
  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • You can export or delete everything from Settings at any time.

Who we are

Heelwise is provided by Wael Rooma ("we", "us"). If you have a privacy question, email wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your phone

Heelwise works without a server. Everything you create is stored in a private database inside the app on your device:

  • Dog profiles: name and life stage, plus breed and birthday if you add them
  • Skill progress, training sessions (duration, repetitions, how it went) and notes
  • Potty logs, behaviour notes (triggers, intensity, any warning signs you tick) and questions for your trainer or vet
  • Your settings and reminder times

We have no access to this data. Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone, so they never pass through a server. The clicker plays a sound built into the app. Heelwise never uses your microphone or camera.

Device backups. If you use iCloud Backup or Google device backup, your phone may include the app's data in that backup. Apple or Google holds that backup under your account, not us.

Sharing you choose. If you create a training summary PDF or an export file, it goes only where you send it, such as to your trainer. After that, whoever receives it is responsible for it.

Data processed to manage your subscription

If you start a trial or subscribe to Heelwise Plus, Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) handles the payment. We never see your card details.

The app uses RevenueCat, Inc. as our service provider to check whether your subscription is active and to restore purchases. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created on your device (not your name or email)
  • your purchase and subscription records from Apple or Google (product, dates, status, country, price)
  • basic technical data such as app version, operating system version and IP address

RevenueCat never receives your dog's logs. It processes this data only on our behalf, under its own privacy policy.

Crash reports

This version of Heelwise does not send crash reports. If we turn them on later, we will update this policy first. Crash reports would contain only technical diagnostics, never your logs.

What we don't do

  • We don't use advertising SDKs, advertising IDs or tracking pixels.
  • We don't send your logs to analytics or to any third party.
  • We don't sell, rent or share personal data, including for targeted advertising.
  • We don't use your data to train AI models.

Legal bases (UK and EU users)

The logs on your device are processed to provide the service you asked for. Subscription data is processed to perform our contract with you and to meet legal obligations such as keeping tax records.

Your rights and choices

  • Access and portability: Settings → Your data → Export gives you all your data as JSON and CSV files.
  • Deletion: Settings → Your data → Delete all data erases everything in the app. Uninstalling the app also removes it from your phone.
  • Subscription data: email us to ask for access to, or deletion of, the records RevenueCat holds. Where the law requires it, we may have to keep some purchase records.
  • Complaints: if you are in the UK or EU, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Retention

Data on your phone stays until you delete it. RevenueCat keeps subscription records for as long as it needs to provide the service and meet legal and tax obligations.

Security

Your phone's own security protects your data: passcode, biometrics and device encryption. We hold no logs on a server, so there is no central database of them to breach.

International transfers

RevenueCat is based in the United States. When subscription data is transferred from the UK or EU, safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses protect it.

Children

Heelwise is meant for adults. Children can train their dog with an adult supervising. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. For important changes, we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
