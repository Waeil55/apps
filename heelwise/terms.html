1. What Heelwise is

Heelwise helps you train your own dog. It gives you reward-based lessons, a clicker, a timer and logs. It is educational. It is not veterinary advice, and it does not replace a qualified trainer or behaviour professional.

Safety first. Stop training and get help if your dog growls, snaps, bites, guards food or objects, panics when left alone, or changes behaviour suddenly. See your vet first, because pain and illness often cause behaviour changes. Then see a certified behaviour professional. When you log these signs, Heelwise will point you to help, but it cannot assess your dog.

2. Training responsibly

  • You are responsible for your dog and for the safety of other people and animals while you train.
  • Follow local laws on leads, recall and public spaces. Until your dog is reliable, train off lead only in safe, enclosed areas.
  • Ask your vet before physical activity if your dog is young, old, unwell or recovering.
  • Use treats that are safe for your dog, and adjust meals so treats don't cause weight gain.
