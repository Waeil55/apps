How to play

What do the numbers mean?

Each number is a run of filled squares in that row or column, in order from left to right or top to bottom. "3 1" means three filled squares in a row, at least one gap, then one more. A row marked 0 has no filled squares.

How do I fill or mark squares?

Tap a square to fill it, or drag along a row or column to fill several at once. Switch to Mark to mark squares you know are empty. Tapping a square again with the same tool clears it.

Will I ever need to guess?

No. Every picture and every generated puzzle is checked by a solver before you see it: it has exactly one solution, and you can reach it by reasoning one row or column at a time. A few pictures start with one to three squares already shown to make that true.

How do hints work?

If a square is wrong, the hint outlines it first. Otherwise it tells you which row or column to look at and reveals one square that can be worked out there.

When does the Daily emblem change?

At midnight on your device's clock. Solve it on consecutive days to grow your streak. Missing a day starts a new streak.

Purchases

What does Hatchwork Premium include?

Five more picture packs (30 pictures) and 15×15 Quick play. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. The Daily emblem, the First Light pack and 5×5 and 10×10 Quick play are free forever.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account, open Hatchwork, then go to Settings → Restore purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store. On iPhone or iPad, use reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use the order in your Google Play account.

Your data

Where is my progress stored?

Only on your device. There is no account or cloud sync. Device backups (iCloud or Google) may include it.

How do I erase everything?

Settings → Reset progress, then tap again to confirm. Deleting the app also removes its data. See the Privacy Policy.
