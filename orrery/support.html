Common questions

Why does my Rising sign differ from another app?

The Rising sign changes about every two hours, so a birth time that is off by even 15 minutes can change it near a boundary. Also check the birth city and AM/PM. Orrery uses Whole Sign houses; other apps may use other house systems, which changes house numbers but not your Rising sign.

I don't know my birth time.

Turn on "I don't know the birth time". Orrery then skips the Rising sign and houses. If the Moon changed sign that day, the app shows both possible Moon signs.

My city isn't listed.

Choose the nearest large city in the same time zone. Within about 50 km the chart is almost always the same.

How accurate are the positions?

Orrery calculates planet positions on your phone with published astronomical formulas. They are accurate to a fraction of a degree, which places each planet in the right sign except when it sits right on a boundary. The app marks those cases.

Are the readings predictions?

No. Orrery is for entertainment and reflection only. Please do not use it for decisions about health, money, law or relationships.

How do I cancel Plus?

iPhone and iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Orrery. Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Orrery. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.

I reinstalled the app or got a new phone.

Open You → Restore purchases to bring back Plus. Birth details are stored only on the device, so you may need to enter them again unless your device backup restored them.

How do I delete my data?

You → Erase all data removes everything from the device. Deleting the app does too. We never receive your birth details.
