Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 is free. Orrery currently has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment or purchase data is processed and the purchase sections below do not apply yet. If paid features are added in a later version, this page will be updated before they launch.

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Orrery app for iPhone, iPad and Android

The short version

  • Birth dates, times and places you enter, for yourself or other people, are stored only on your phone. We never receive them.
  • Charts and readings are calculated on the device. No birth data is sent to any server or astrology service.
  • There is no Orrery account. We never ask for your email or phone number.
  • There are no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking, and we never sell data.
  • If you subscribe to Plus, Apple or Google process the payment. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, sees an anonymous ID and your purchase status, and nothing about your chart.
  • You can delete everything at any time in You → Erase all data.

Who we are

Orrery is made by Waeil Mikhaeil ("we", "us"). Contact: wael.rooma@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your own birth details, the people you add (a name plus their birth date, optional time and city) and your settings are saved in the app's private storage. They are used only to calculate charts, readings and compatibility on the device. Your device's own backup (iCloud or Google device backup) may include this data in your private backup if you have backup turned on. That backup is controlled by your Apple or Google account, not by us.

When you add someone else's birth details, please make sure they would be happy for you to do so. The details never leave your phone unless you share a reading yourself.

Birth places are chosen from a city list built into the app. Orrery does not ask for or use your device location.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your device by the operating system. The reminder text is generic and contains no personal information. No push service or server is involved.

What is processed off the device

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

We process subscription data because it is needed to perform our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not receive the birth details on your device, so we are not their controller.

Children

Orrery is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data. Because we receive no birth details at all, nothing a child enters reaches us.

Retention and deletion

On-device data stays until you delete it. You can remove any person you added, or use You → Erase all data. Deleting the app also removes it. RevenueCat keeps purchase records as long as needed for subscriptions, tax and accounting. To ask us to delete that record, email wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to processing. Because we hold almost nothing, most requests are handled by the delete controls in the app. For anything else, email us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Data is stored inside the app's private storage on your device and protected by your device's lock and encryption. Connections to the app stores and to RevenueCat use encryption in transit.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. If the change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
