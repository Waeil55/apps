Common questions

The camera shows a black screen or asks for permission again

Squarely needs camera access to scan. On iPhone open Settings → Squarely → Camera; on Android open Settings → Apps → Squarely → Permissions → Camera. You can also scan from a photo with the photo button.

A code won't scan

Hold the phone 10 to 20 cm away so the whole code fits inside the frame, and avoid glare. In low light, tap the light button. Very small, damaged or low-contrast codes may not read; try a closer photo or a screenshot with "From photo".

Why didn't the link open by itself?

On purpose. Squarely shows you the real website name first and points out warning signs. Tap "Open link" when you are happy. Links with warning signs open the details screen first, where "Open anyway" is still available.

Is a link safe if Squarely shows no warnings?

Not necessarily. The check looks for common tricks, but a website with a normal-looking name can still be a scam. Only open links from people and places you trust, and never type a password into a site you reached from a code you weren't expecting.

How do I join a Wi-Fi network from a code?

Phones don't let apps join networks silently. Tap "Copy password", then choose the network in your phone's Wi-Fi settings and paste. The iPhone and Android camera apps can also join Wi-Fi codes directly.

Where is my history stored? Can I get it back after deleting the app?

Only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes your history, unless your phone restores the app from its own device backup. Squarely has no account or cloud copy.

Free shows only my latest 50 items. Is the rest gone?

No. Older items stay on your phone; Free shows the latest 50 plus everything you star. Squarely Plus shows your full history with search. Nothing is deleted unless you delete it.

How do I restore or cancel Squarely Plus?

Restore: Settings → Plan → Restore. Cancel: in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions; cancel at least 24 hours before renewal. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.
