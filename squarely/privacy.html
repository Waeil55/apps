Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 is free. Squarely currently has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment or purchase data is processed and the purchase sections below do not apply yet. If paid features are added in a later version, this page will be updated before they launch.

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Squarely app for iPhone, iPad and Android

The short version

  • The camera is used only to find codes. Camera frames are processed on your phone and are never saved or sent.
  • Your scan history and the codes you create are stored only on your phone. We never receive them. You can turn history off.
  • There is no Squarely account, no advertising, no analytics SDK and no tracking.
  • If you subscribe, Apple or Google handles the payment. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives an anonymous id and your purchase receipt, nothing about your scans.

Who we are

Squarely is published by Wael Rooma ("we", "us"). Contact: wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Links and web searches

Squarely never opens a link by itself. When you tap "Open link", the website opens in your phone's in-app browser and that website's own privacy policy applies. "Search the web" for a product barcode opens a DuckDuckGo search for the barcode number in that browser. Squarely's link check runs entirely on your device; it does not send links to us or to any checking service.

Subscriptions

Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to check whether you have an active Squarely Plus subscription. RevenueCat receives a random app user id, your store receipt, the app version and your country and currency. It does not receive scans, created codes or settings. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no email collection, no contacts access, no location, no microphone.
  • No advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics, no data brokers, no selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No uploading of what you scan. No "AI" processing.

Backups

The app's database may be included in your phone's own encrypted device backup (iCloud or Google). That backup is controlled by you and your device provider.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your history: Settings → Delete history removes every scan and created code from the device. Single items can be deleted from their detail screen.
  • Stop keeping history: Settings → Save scans to History. Scans are then shown but not stored.
  • Turn off camera access: in your phone's Settings at any time. You can still scan from a photo.
  • Uninstall: removes everything Squarely stored.

Because we hold no personal data about you on our servers, access or deletion requests to us usually have nothing to return. For subscription data held by Apple, Google or RevenueCat, we will help you contact them. Residents of the EEA, UK and California have rights of access, deletion and objection; write to wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Children

Squarely is a general tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app before they apply.
