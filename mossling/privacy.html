Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 is free. Mossling currently has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment or purchase data is processed and the purchase sections below do not apply yet. If paid features are added in a later version, this page will be updated before they launch.

Effective 13 November 2026

  • Mossling is made for children aged 3 to 5 and their grown-ups.
  • We do not collect personal information from children. There is no account, no name, no photo and no birthday.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.
  • Learning progress, stars, play time and settings stay on the device.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google, with RevenueCat checking whether a subscription is active.

Who we are

Mossling is published by Waeil Mikhaeil. Contact us at wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Information stored on the device

To work, the app keeps a small file on the device with: the age band a grown-up chose (3, 4 or 5), which letters, numbers and shapes have been traced or answered correctly, stars earned per day, minutes played per day for the last few weeks, and settings (appearance, haptics, spoken prompts, daily play limit). This information never leaves the device and we cannot see it. Deleting the app, or using Grown-ups → Reset progress, removes it.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, photos, voice recordings, contacts, location, advertising identifiers or device identifiers, and we do not use cookies, analytics or advertising networks in the app. The app requests no device permissions.

Purchases

Mossling Plus is sold through the App Store or Google Play and is only offered in the grown-ups area, behind a parental gate. Apple or Google processes the payment; we never see card details. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to confirm whether a subscription is active. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user id and the purchase record from the store (product, dates, status). It does not receive any information about your child or their learning. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children's privacy (COPPA and GDPR-K)

Mossling is designed for children and does not collect personal information from them. Purchases, links outside the app, support email and settings are behind a parental gate that young children cannot pass. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

Email support

If a grown-up emails us, we use the email address and message only to reply, and delete the conversation within months of it ending.

Your rights

Because learning data stays on the device, you control it directly. To ask about or delete the anonymous purchase record held by RevenueCat, email wael.rooma@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have further rights to access, correct or delete data; contact us to use them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and describe material changes in the app's release notes.
