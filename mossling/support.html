Common questions

How do I open the grown-ups area?

Tap Grown-ups at the top right of the home screen, then tap the three numbers shown as words (for example "four, seven, two") on the keypad. This keeps purchases, links and settings away from little hands.

How do I set a daily play limit?

Grown-ups → Daily play limit. When time is up, your child sees a calm "time for a rest" screen. A grown-up can add ten more minutes through the gate.

Which age should I choose?

Choose your child's age. Age 3 gets bigger tracing guides and three choices; age 5 gets finer guides, four choices and lowercase letter matching. You can change it any time in the grown-ups area.

Where is my child's progress stored?

Only on the device. There's no account and we never receive it. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I restore Mossling Plus?

Grown-ups → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with. With Family Sharing on, other family devices can use it too.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Mossling. On Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Mossling. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple or Google, so refunds are handled by them: reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, or the Google Play order history for Android.

Does Mossling speak?

On the web preview it can read prompts aloud with the device's own voice. Spoken prompts on iPhone, iPad and Android are coming in an update; every activity already works from pictures alone.
