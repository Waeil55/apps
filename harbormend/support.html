Common questions

How do I play?

Tap a pile, basket, kettle, tin or cart on the board to gather a piece. Drag a piece onto a matching one (same item, same level) to mend them into the next item up: two driftwood make a plank, two planks make a plank stack, and so on. Neighbours at the top ask for pieces. When you have them, tap Deliver to earn shells. Spend shells in the Harbour to restore Saltmere Cove, one spot at a time. You can also play by tapping: tap a piece, then tap a matching piece or an empty space.

Is there energy or a timer?

No. Gather as much as you like, whenever you like. The only limit is space on the board. If it fills up, merge pieces, deliver an order, or select a piece and tap Put away (you can undo it straight after).

What is free, and what does the unlock add?

Chapters 1 and 2 of the story (the old pier and the tea hut) and endless orders on the board are free forever. The one-time Harbormend unlock adds chapters 3 to 6: the boat shed, the painted steps, the sea wall and the lighthouse, with four more neighbours and the stone cart. There is no subscription and nothing else to buy.

I bought the unlock on another device. How do I get it back?

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account. Then go to Settings → Restore purchase, or tap Restore purchase on the unlock screen. A purchase made through Apple doesn't carry over to Google, or the other way round.

How do I get a refund?

Apple and Google handle all refunds. For Apple, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google, go to play.google.com, open Order history and request a refund.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Your progress is stored only on your device, because there is no account. A full device backup and restore through iCloud or Google usually brings it across. You can always restore the purchase, even if your progress doesn't come across.

Accessibility options

Settings has Reduce motion (it also follows your system setting) and switches for sound and haptics. Text follows your system text size. Every space on the board is labelled for VoiceOver and TalkBack with the piece and its level, and every piece shows its level as a number, so nothing depends on colour alone. You can play entirely by tapping.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Start over erases your harbour and starts a new game. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. We keep no gameplay data on any server. See the Privacy Policy.
