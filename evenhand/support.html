Getting started

Do I need an account?

No. Tap Get started, enter your name, the other parent's name if you like (it is only a label) and your children's names. Everything stays on your phone.

Does the other parent see what I enter?

No. Evenhand 1.0 doesn't send anything to anyone. If you want someone to see a record, export it from More › Records & export and choose where to send it.

Does it cost anything?

No. Version 1.0 is free, with every feature included. There are no subscriptions, in-app purchases or ads.

Journal and records

Can I edit or delete a journal entry?

No. Each entry is sealed when you save it, which is what makes the journal trustworthy. If you made a mistake, add a short correction as a new entry.

What does "chain intact" mean?

Each entry carries a SHA-256 hash of its contents and of the entry before it. Your phone recomputes every hash. "Intact" means no stored entry has been changed, removed or reordered since it was saved.

How can someone else check an exported record?

Each entry hash is SHA-256 of evenhand-msg-v1|space id|entry number|author id|time sealed|SHA-256 of entry text|previous hash. The first entry's previous hash is 64 zeros. Anyone can recompute these with a standard SHA-256 tool, using the values printed in the PDF or the JSON export. The export digest is SHA-256 of the exported records in canonical JSON.

Will a court accept Evenhand records?

We can't promise that, and nobody else honestly can either. Evenhand keeps factual, time-stamped records with integrity hashes and makes them easy to export. Whether and how they are used is up to the people and courts involved. Please ask your lawyer.

Calendar and expenses

How do I record a change to the schedule?

When you and the other parent agree to change days, open Calendar, pick a day and tap Record a change. Choose the days and who has the children, and add a note if you like. The change shows on your calendar straight away. If it falls through, open it and tap Cancel this change: it stays in the history, marked as cancelled.

How does the expense balance work?

Log an expense with the share you agreed (50% by default). Evenhand adds the other parent's share to a running balance. When it has been paid outside the app, open the expense and tap Mark as settled. Evenhand doesn't move money.

Your data

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone, in the app's private storage. We don't run a server for Evenhand 1.0 and never receive your records.

Export everything

Go to Settings › Export all data, or More › Records & export › All data (JSON). PDF records are on the same Records screen.

Moving to a new phone

Your records live on this phone, so keep regular exports. Your phone's own backup (iCloud Backup or Google backup) may also include the app's data if you have it turned on.

Delete everything

Go to Settings › Delete all data. This erases your journal, calendar, agreed changes, expenses, receipt photos and children's details from the app. There is no other copy, so it can't be undone. We can't delete or recover anything for you, because we never had it.
