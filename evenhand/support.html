Getting started

How do I invite my co-parent?

Open More › Family & invite and tap Share invite. Send the link privately, for example by text or email. Your co-parent installs Evenhand, chooses "I have an invite from my co-parent" and opens or pastes the link. The link works once and expires after 14 days.

Why shouldn't I post the invite link publicly?

The link carries the key that encrypts your family's sensitive records. Anyone with an unused link could join in your co-parent's place, so share it only with them.

Does my co-parent have to pay?

No. Everyone can use messaging, the calendar, swaps, expenses and the children's card for free. If either parent subscribes to Plus, it covers both of you.

Messages and records

Can I edit or delete a message?

No, and neither can your co-parent or we. That's what makes the record trustworthy. If you made a mistake, send a short correction.

What does "chain intact" mean?

Each message carries a SHA-256 hash of its contents and of the message before it. Your phone recomputes every hash. "Intact" means no stored message has been changed, removed or reordered since it was sent.

How can someone else check an exported record?

Each message hash is SHA-256 of evenhand-msg-v1|family id|sequence|sender id|server time|SHA-256 of message text|previous hash. The first message's previous hash is 64 zeros. Anyone can recompute these with a standard SHA-256 tool, using the values printed in the PDF or the JSON export. The export digest is SHA-256 of the exported records in canonical JSON.

Will a court accept Evenhand records?

We can't promise that, and nobody else honestly can either. Evenhand keeps factual, time-stamped records with integrity hashes and makes them easy to export. Whether and how they are used is up to the people and courts involved. Please ask your lawyer.

Calendar and expenses

How do swaps work?

Pick the days on the calendar and send a request with a reason. Your co-parent approves or declines it once, and you can withdraw it until then. Approved swaps show on both calendars, and every request stays in Swap history.

How do reimbursements work?

Log an expense with the share you agreed (50% by default) and request reimbursement. Your co-parent marks it paid once they have paid you some other way. Evenhand doesn't move money. They can also decline with a note.

Subscriptions

How do I cancel?

On iPhone, go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Evenhand. On Android, go to Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid for, and your records always stay.

Restoring a purchase on a new phone

Sign in, then tap Settings › Restore purchases.

Your data

Go to Settings › Export my data, or Records › All data (JSON). This is free, always.

Delete my account
