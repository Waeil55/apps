Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to Evenhand version 1.0 for iPhone, iPad and Android

The short version

  • Everything you enter in Evenhand (your journal, the custody calendar, agreed changes, expenses, receipt photos and your children's details) is kept only on your phone, in the app's private storage.
  • There is no account and no sign-in. Evenhand 1.0 has no server, and nothing you enter is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • No ads, no advertising IDs, no tracking, no analytics and no crash reporting.
  • You decide what to export and where it goes, using your phone's share sheet.
  • Settings › Delete all data, or deleting the app, erases your records. We have no copy, so we can't recover them.

Who we are

Evenhand is provided by Waeil Mikhaeil ("we", "us"). Contact us at wael.rooma@gmail.com with any privacy question.

What the app keeps, and where

Evenhand stores what you enter so the app can show it back to you. All of it stays in the app's storage on your phone. None of it reaches us.

The tone check and the integrity hashes (SHA-256) are computed on your phone. The app asks for access to your photos or camera only when you choose to attach a receipt, and you can say no.

What leaves your phone

Nothing, unless you export it. When you export records (More › Records & export, or Settings › Export all data), Evenhand creates a PDF or JSON file on your phone and opens the share sheet. You choose where the file goes, for example to your files, to the other parent, a mediator or a lawyer. Once you share a file, the app or service you send it with handles it under its own terms.

Your phone's own backup (for example iCloud Backup or Google backup), if you have it turned on, may include the app's data under Apple's or Google's terms. We never receive those backups.

Who processes data for us

No one. Evenhand 1.0 does not use a hosting provider, a sign-in service, a subscription service, analytics or crash reporting, so no third party receives your records or information about how you use the app. Version 1.0 is free and has no in-app purchases.

What we don't do

  • We don't use advertising SDKs, advertising IDs or tracking pixels.
  • We don't see, read or monitor anything you write. Evenhand is not an emergency service and does not alert anyone. If you or your children are in danger, contact local emergency services.
  • We don't use your records to train AI models.
  • We don't sell, rent or share data, because we don't have any of it.

Requests from courts, lawyers and authorities

We don't hold your records, so we have nothing to hand over. Any export of your records is made by you, from the app.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

Your records stay on your phone until you delete them. Settings › Delete all data erases the journal, calendar, agreed changes, expenses, receipt photos, children's details and preferences from the app. Deleting the app does the same. There is no other copy, so this can't be undone. Export your data first if you want to keep a copy.

Because your data only exists on your phone, a lost, reset or replaced phone means the records are gone unless you kept an export or a device backup.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data. You can do all of that yourself in the app: view and edit your records, export them (Settings › Export all data) and delete them (Settings › Delete all data). We hold no personal data from the app about you. If you email us for support, we use your email address and message only to reply, and you can ask us to delete that correspondence.

Children

Evenhand is for parents and guardians aged 18 or older. Children don't use the app. Parents enter information about their children, and it stays on the parent's phone as described above.

Security

Your records are protected by your phone's own security: the app's private storage, your device's encryption and your passcode, Face ID or fingerprint. Anyone who can unlock your phone and open the app can read your records, so keep a passcode on your phone. Journal entries carry integrity hashes, so any change to the stored journal shows up when it is checked.

Future versions

If a later version offers optional sharing between two parents, it will be something you choose to turn on, and we will update this policy, explaining what is stored and where, before it is available.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date and tell you in the app before material changes take effect.
