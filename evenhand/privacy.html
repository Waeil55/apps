Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Evenhand app for iPhone, iPad and Android

The short version

  • Evenhand stores the records you and your co-parent create (messages, calendar, expenses, your children's details) so both of you can see them on your phones.
  • The most sensitive parts, including message text and your children's health and insurance details, are encrypted on your phone with a key we never receive.
  • No ads, no advertising IDs, no tracking across apps or websites, and no third-party analytics.
  • We do not sell or rent your data, and we never will.
  • Records are shared between two parents. Something you send to your co-parent stays in their copy of the record, even if you leave.

How the encryption works

When a family is created, the app makes a random encryption key on the parent's phone. The key reaches the other parent only inside the invite link the first parent shares. Our servers never receive it. Encrypted fields use XChaCha20-Poly1305 and are unreadable to us and to our hosting provider.

One trade-off: so that anyone can check a record later, each message's integrity hash is computed from the message text. The server stores that hash. A hash does not reveal the text, but someone who already knows the exact wording of a message could confirm that it was sent.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase hosts the database, sign-in and file storage, in the United States (East) region. It acts only on our instructions.
  • RevenueCat checks whether your family's subscription is active. It receives a random user ID, purchase records from Apple or Google, and basic technical data. It never receives your messages or family records.
  • Apple and Google handle payments. We never see your card details.
  • Crash reporting: this version does not send crash reports. If we turn it on later, we will update this policy first, and reports will contain technical diagnostics only, never your records.

What we don't do

  • We don't use advertising SDKs, advertising IDs or tracking pixels.
  • We don't read, scan or moderate your messages. Evenhand does not monitor conversations or alert anyone. If you or your children are in danger, contact local emergency services.
  • We don't use your records to train AI models. The tone check runs entirely on your phone.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your data for marketing.

Sharing with your co-parent

Evenhand is a shared space for exactly two parents. Everything in the family (messages, calendar, swaps, expenses and the children's card) is visible to both of you. This is how the app works, and you can't make it private from the other parent.

Requests from courts, lawyers and authorities

We give data to a third party only when the law requires it, for example a valid court order or subpoena. Encrypted fields stay encrypted, because we do not have the key. Where the law allows, we will tell you about the request first. Parents can export their own records at any time from the app.

How long we keep data

Records are kept while the family exists, so both parents keep an unchanged history. If you leave a family, your account is closed, but messages and records you created stay in your co-parent's copy, because removing them would change their record. Once both parents have deleted their accounts, we delete the family's records within 30 days, and they leave our backups within a further 35 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict processing. You can export your records yourself in the app (Settings › Export my data). For other requests, email wael.rooma@gmail.com. We will answer within 30 days.

Deletion has one limit. Records you sent to your co-parent also belong to their record. We will delete your account and personal data, but the messages and entries you shared stay in your co-parent's history, under your display name.

If you're in the EEA or UK, our legal basis is performance of our contract with you, to provide the service, and our legitimate interest in keeping records intact and secure. You can complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Evenhand is for parents and guardians aged 18 or older. Children don't use the app. Parents enter information about their children, and that information is protected as described above.

Security

We use encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. We also encrypt sensitive fields on the device, use row-level access rules that let only the two members of a family read its records, and use database rules that block editing or deleting sent messages. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date and tell you in the app before material changes take effect.
