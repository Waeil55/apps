1. Who can use Evenhand

2. What Evenhand does

Evenhand gives two co-parents a shared space for messages, a custody calendar with swap requests, expenses and reimbursement requests, and a children's information card. It also offers exports of those records.

  • Messages are permanent. Once sent, a message cannot be edited or deleted by either parent, or by us at your request, because that would break the record for the other parent.
  • Decisions are final in the app. A swap or reimbursement request can be decided once. Later changes are made with new requests, and the history is kept.
  • Integrity hashes show whether stored messages have changed since they were sent. They do not prove who physically typed a message, whether a message is true, or what it means.
  • The tone check is an optional, automatic suggestion that runs on your phone. It can be wrong, and you decide what to send.
  • Calendar and money figures come from what the parents enter. Evenhand does not decide custody, enforce a parenting plan, or move money between parents.
