2. What Evenhand does

Evenhand 1.0 is a private organiser that runs on your phone. It keeps a custody calendar with the changes you record, events, expenses with agreed splits and a running balance, your children's details, and a dated journal. It also exports those records as PDF or JSON files. It does not send anything to the other parent or to anyone else.

  • Journal entries are permanent. Once saved, an entry can't be edited or deleted on its own. Delete all data in Settings erases everything at once.
  • Integrity hashes show whether stored journal entries have changed since they were saved. They do not prove who typed an entry, whether it is true, or what it means.
  • Agreed changes and settlements are your own record of what was agreed or paid outside the app. The other parent does not see or confirm them in Evenhand.
  • The tone check is an optional, automatic suggestion that runs on your phone. It can be wrong, and you decide what to save.
  • Calendar and money figures come from what you enter. Evenhand does not decide custody, enforce a parenting plan, or move money.
