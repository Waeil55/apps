Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 is free. Mathwick currently has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment or purchase data is processed and the purchase sections below do not apply yet. If paid features are added in a later version, this page will be updated before they launch.

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Mathwick app for iPhone, iPad and Android

The short version

  • Mathwick is a maths game for children aged 6 to 10. It collects no personal information from children or anyone else.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, no chat and no way to contact other people.
  • There are no ads, no advertising IDs, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking.
  • Progress, nicknames and settings stay only on your device.
  • The only network use is checking and restoring the optional one-time purchase, which only a grown-up can reach.

Who we are

Mathwick is provided by Wael Rooma ("we", "us"). Contact us at wael.rooma@gmail.com with any privacy question.

Data that stays on your device

Mathwick works offline. It stores the following in its private storage on your device:

  • Explorer profiles: an optional nickname (it can be left blank or made up) and a lantern colour. We suggest a nickname rather than a full name.
  • Progress: lanterns lit, stars, and counts of answers per skill, used for the grown-ups progress report.
  • Settings: sound, vibration and reduced motion.

We have no access to this data, and it is never sent to us or to anyone else. The only way it leaves your device is a device backup (iCloud Backup or Google device backup) that you control under your own account.

The grown-ups area and parental gate

Settings, the progress report, the purchase screen and every link that leaves the app (including this page) sit behind a parental gate: an adult types the answer to a multiplication written in words. Children cannot reach a purchase or the web without a grown-up.

The optional purchase

"Mathwick Full Valley" is a one-time purchase. Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) handles the payment, and we never see your payment details. Store parental controls such as Apple's Ask to Buy and Google Play purchase approvals also apply.

To confirm the unlock and restore it on your other devices, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. as our service provider. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created on the device (not a name, email or advertising ID)
  • the Mathwick purchase records from Apple or Google: product, date, status, country and price
  • basic technical data needed to deliver the service, such as app version, operating system version and IP address

RevenueCat processes this data only on our behalf to provide the purchase, under its data processing terms. It receives none of the profiles or progress described above, and we do not use it for analytics or advertising.

What we don't do

  • No advertising of any kind, and no advertising SDKs or IDs.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services.
  • No notifications, no camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data, and no use of data to train AI models.
  • No links to social networks and no user-generated content.

Children's privacy

Mathwick is designed for children and is built to follow the Apple App Store Kids category rules and Google Play's Families policy. Because we do not collect personal information from children, there is nothing for a parent to request from us about their child's use of the game. Parents can review progress in the grown-ups area and erase it at any time.

Legal bases (UK and EU users)

We process purchase data through RevenueCat to perform our contract with you (providing the unlock you bought) and to meet legal obligations such as keeping tax records. We do not process gameplay data: it stays on your device.

Your rights and choices

  • Deletion: Grown-ups → "Reset this explorer's progress" or "Remove explorer" erases data on the device. Deleting the app removes everything.
  • Purchase records: email us to ask for access to, or deletion of, the records held by RevenueCat. We may need to keep some purchase records where the law requires.
  • Complaints: in the UK or EU you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Retention

Data on the device stays until you erase it or delete the app. RevenueCat keeps purchase records as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal and tax obligations.

International transfers

RevenueCat is based in the United States. When purchase data is transferred from the UK or EU, appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses protect it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. We will not add advertising, analytics or any collection of children's data.
