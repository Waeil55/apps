For grown-ups

How do I open the grown-ups area?

Tap the grown-ups button at the top right of the map. Type the answer to the multiplication written in words. After two wrong answers the gate closes and returns to the map.

What is free, and what does Full Valley add?

Lantern Meadow (adding and taking away to 10, number pairs) and Willow Brook (to 20, doubles, comparing, missing numbers) are free forever: 16 lanterns. Full Valley is a one-time purchase that adds Mill Hill (place value and numbers to 100), Starlit Orchard and Crystal Hollow (times tables and division) and Summit Observatory (numbers to 1,000 and mixed review), plus up to four explorer profiles.

Is there a subscription?

No. Full Valley is bought once and kept. There are no ads and nothing else to buy.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, open Grown-ups and tap "Restore purchase".

My child is finding it too easy or too hard.

Every land opens at its first lantern, so an older child can start in a later land. Within a trail, Mathwick eases the next puzzle after a miss and shows a picture hint (dots, arrays, or tens and ones).

Can siblings play on one device?

With Full Valley you can add up to four explorers. Each keeps their own lanterns, stars and report. Tap the lantern at the top left of the map to switch.

How do I erase progress?

Grown-ups → choose the explorer → "Reset this explorer's progress". Removing an explorer erases their data. This cannot be undone.

Does Mathwick collect data about my child?

No. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Progress stays on the device. See the Privacy Policy.
