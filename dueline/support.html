Dueline: subscriptions and bills, before they charge

What is the difference between a subscription and a bill?

A subscription renews on its own, so Dueline simply moves it to the next date. A bill stays due until you tap the tick to mark it paid; if the date passes first, it shows as overdue.

How are the totals worked out?

Left to pay this month adds up charges from today to the end of the month, plus any unpaid bills. Per month is an average: yearly items count as one twelfth, weekly items as 52 ÷ 12 charges.

How do free trials work?

Turn on Free trial and set the date the trial turns paid. Dueline reminds you before that date, so you can cancel in time.

I am not getting reminders

Check that Settings → Renewal reminders is on and that notifications are allowed for Dueline in your phone’s settings. Reminders arrive at 9:00 on the day you choose.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. There is no account, so deleting the app deletes your list. Dueline Plus can export everything to a CSV file you can keep.

Subscriptions and restoring purchases

Dueline Plus unlocks unlimited items and CSV export. On a new phone, open Settings → Restore purchases. To cancel, use your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
