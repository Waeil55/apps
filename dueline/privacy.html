Privacy policy

Short version: the subscriptions and bills you add stay on your device. There is no account, no bank connection, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We never see your list.

Who we are

Dueline is published by Wael Rooma. Contact: wael.rooma@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

  • The items you add: names, amounts, billing cycles, dates, categories, notes, and whether a bill is paid or a subscription is cancelled.
  • Your settings: currency, reminder timing and appearance.

This data is stored locally in the app and is never uploaded to us or anyone else. Deleting the app, or Settings → Delete all data, removes it.

Reminders

Renewal reminders are local notifications scheduled by your phone. No push service or server is involved, and nothing about them is sent to us.

Export

If you use Export to CSV, the file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to send or save it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat, Inc. to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, purchase receipts and basic device information (such as the operating system and app version), never the items in your list. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy at revenuecat.com. We never see your payment card details.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in, no bank or card connection, and no name, email, location, contacts, camera or microphone access.
  • No advertising, no advertising ID, and no selling or sharing of data for advertising.
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in this version.

Email

If you email support, we use your message and address only to answer you, and we delete them when they are no longer needed.

Children

Dueline is meant for adults managing their own payments. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Your list never leaves your device, so you control it directly. For questions about purchase data held by RevenueCat, Apple or Google, or to exercise rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, contact wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date.
