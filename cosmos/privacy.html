Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 is free. Cosmos Incremental currently has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment or purchase data is processed and the purchase sections below do not apply yet. If paid features are added in a later version, this page will be updated before they launch.

Effective 13 November 2026. This is a draft.

  • Cosmos Incremental has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • If you buy Cosmos Premium, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google, with RevenueCat checking it for us using an anonymous ID.

Who we are

Cosmos Incremental ("Cosmos", "the app") is made by Wael Rooma. Contact us at wael.rooma@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your stardust, machines, research, neutron stars and settings (theme, haptics, motion) are saved in the app's local storage on your phone or tablet. We never receive this data. Deleting the app deletes it. You can also erase it at any time in Settings → Start over.

Purchases

Cosmos Premium is a one-time in-app purchase sold by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your name, email address or payment details. To confirm that a purchase is valid and to let you restore it on a new device, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, the purchase receipt from the store, and basic device information (such as the operating system version and app version). See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Ads and rewarded boosts

This version of Cosmos contains no advertising SDK and shows no ads. If a future version offers optional rewarded boosts, we will update this policy before that version ships, and boosts will always be optional.

Children

Cosmos is a general-audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For purchase records held by Apple, Google or RevenueCat, contact us and we will help you reach them. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have further rights.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and describe the change in the app's release notes.
