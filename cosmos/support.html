Common questions

How do I start?

Tap the star to gather stardust. At 15 stardust, buy a Dust Probe. From then on your system gathers by itself, even while the app is closed.

How much do I get while I'm away?

Your machines keep gathering at their full rate for up to 8 hours away. The Long Exposure research and the Dense Core and Galactic Halo perks extend that limit.

What does Supernova do?

Once you have gathered 9 million stardust in a cycle, you can collapse your system. Stardust, machines and research reset. In return you earn neutron stars, and each one permanently adds 5% to all production. Reaching certain totals of neutron stars unlocks permanent perks.

What is orbital resonance?

Owning 25, 50, 100, 150, 200, 300, 400 or 500 of one machine doubles that machine's output each time. Small moons appear on its orbit to show it.

What does Premium include?

A one-time purchase that doubles every source of stardust (taps, machines and time away) and unlocks the Nebula, Aurora and Eclipse themes. It is not a subscription. The free game is complete without it.

I bought Premium on another phone

Open Settings → Restore purchase while signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account.

Is my progress backed up?

No. Progress is saved only on your device, so deleting the app deletes it. Device backups made by iCloud or Google may include it.

Can I turn off animations or vibration?

Yes, in Settings. Reduce motion also follows your device's accessibility setting by default.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are refunded by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play (play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory) under their policies.
