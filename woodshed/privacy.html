Woodshed

Privacy policy

Version 1.0 is free. Woodshed currently has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment or purchase data is processed and the purchase sections below do not apply yet. If paid features are added in a later version, this page will be updated before they launch.

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Woodshed app for iPhone, iPad and Android

The short version

  • Your practice log, routines and settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them.
  • There is no Woodshed account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • The tuner uses the microphone only while the tuner is on screen and switched on. Sound is analyzed in memory to find its pitch and immediately discarded. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • If you subscribe to Plus, Apple or Google process the payment. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, sees an anonymous ID and your purchase status, and nothing about your practice.

Who we are

Woodshed is made by Wael Rooma ("we", "us"). Contact: wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

Practice sessions (date, length, focus, piece names, tempo, rating and notes), routines and settings live in a local database inside the app. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Your device's own backup (iCloud or Google device backup) may include this data in your private backup if you have backup turned on; that backup is controlled by your Apple or Google account, not by us.

Microphone

Woodshed asks for microphone access the first time you start the tuner. Audio is processed on your device, a fraction of a second at a time, to estimate the note you are playing. It is never written to storage, never recorded and never transmitted. The microphone switches off when you stop the tuner or leave the tuner screen. You can withdraw access at any time in your device settings; everything except the tuner keeps working.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold through the App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) to confirm whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID, your device platform, the products you bought and their status. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com. Apple and Google handle your payment details under their own policies; we never see them.

Children

Woodshed is a general-audience practice tool and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Nothing you enter leaves the device.

Keeping and deleting data

On-device data stays until you delete it. You can delete a single session or a routine, or use Settings → Erase all data. Deleting the app also removes it. To ask about the purchase record RevenueCat keeps, email wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we do not hold your practice data, the simplest way to exercise them is in the app itself. For anything else, contact us and we will reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, mention it in the app's release notes.
