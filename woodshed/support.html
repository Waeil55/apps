The tuner shows nothing

Allow microphone access for Woodshed in your device settings, then tap Start tuner. Play one note at a time, close to the phone, in a quiet room. Very low notes (below about 40 Hz) may need a moment to settle.

The tuner reads a little differently from another tuner

Check the reference pitch in Settings → Tuner reference (A4). Most music uses 440 Hz; some orchestras use 442 Hz.

I can't hear the metronome

Turn up the media volume. On iPhone the metronome plays even with the silent switch on. Bluetooth headphones add a small delay; wired headphones or the speaker are most precise.

How does the streak work?

Any practice on a day keeps your streak going, and today doesn't break it until the day is over. The daily goal fills the ring but isn't required for the streak.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings → Woodshed Plus → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with.

Cancel Plus

iPhone and iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Move my log to a new phone

Your log lives on your device and moves with an iCloud or Google device backup restore.
