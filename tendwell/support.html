Common questions

How do streaks work?

A daily habit's streak counts the days in a row you kept it, skipping the days it is not scheduled. Today never breaks a streak while it is still open. A weekly habit's streak counts the weeks in a row you reached its target, such as 3 times a week.

I forgot to check off a habit yesterday. Can I fix it?

Yes. Open the habit and tap the day in its History. Tap it again to undo.

What is the difference between daily and weekly habits?

Daily habits are due on the weekdays you pick, for example every day or Monday to Friday. Weekly habits are done any days you like, a set number of times each week.

Why did my reminder not appear?

Check that notifications are allowed for Tendwell in your phone's Settings, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is not silencing them. On Android, some battery savers delay notifications; allow Tendwell to run without restrictions. Reminders are not available in a web browser.

Can I change which day my week starts on?

Yes. Go to Settings → Week starts on, and choose Monday or Sunday. Weekly targets and the heatmap follow your choice.

How do I move my habits to a new phone?

Tendwell has no account, so your habits move only with your phone's own backup or transfer (iCloud backup, Quick Start, or Google device backup). To restore Plus, open Settings → Tendwell Plus → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account.

How do I cancel Plus?

On iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android, go to Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Your habits stay, and the free features keep working.

How do I delete my data?

To delete one habit and its history, open it and tap Delete habit. To delete everything, go to Settings → Erase all data.
