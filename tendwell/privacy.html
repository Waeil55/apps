Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 is free. Tendwell currently has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment or purchase data is processed and the purchase sections below do not apply yet. If paid features are added in a later version, this page will be updated before they launch.

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Tendwell app for iPhone, iPad and Android

The short version

  • Your habits are stored only on your phone, and we never receive them. That includes habit names, icons, schedules, check-ins, reminder times and settings.
  • There is no Tendwell account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself. We run no notification server.
  • There are no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking, and we never sell data.
  • If you subscribe to Plus, Apple or Google process the payment. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, sees an anonymous ID and your purchase status, and nothing about your habits.
  • You can delete everything at any time in Settings → Erase all data.

Who we are

Tendwell is made by Waeil Mikhaeil ("we", "us"). Contact: wael.rooma@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your habits, check-ins, reminder times and settings are stored in a local database inside the app. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Your device's own backup system (iCloud or Google device backup) may include this data in your private backup, if you have backup turned on. That backup is controlled by your Apple or Google account, not by us.

If you turn on a reminder, the app asks your phone for permission to show notifications and schedules them on the phone. The reminder shows the habit's name and icon on your lock screen, so choose names you are comfortable seeing there.

What is processed off the device

We do not use RevenueCat's attribution or advertising integrations, and we do not link your purchase to your identity.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

We process subscription data because it is needed to perform our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not receive the habits on your device, so we are not their controller.

Children

Tendwell is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Retention and deletion

On-device data stays until you delete it. You can delete a single habit with its history, or use Settings → Erase all data. Deleting the app also removes it. RevenueCat keeps purchase records as long as needed for subscriptions, tax and accounting. To ask us to delete that record, email wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to processing. Because we hold almost nothing, most requests are handled by the delete controls in the app. For anything else, email us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Data is stored inside the app's private storage on your device and protected by your device's lock and encryption. Connections to the app stores and to RevenueCat use encryption in transit.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. If the change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
