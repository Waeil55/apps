Common questions

Where is my journal stored?

Only on your device. There is no account and no cloud copy. To move to a new phone, use your device backup or Settings > Export.

How do I restore Lamplight Plus on a new device?

Open Settings > Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone and iPad: Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Android: Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

My reminder didn't arrive.

Check that notifications for Lamplight are allowed in your device settings, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't hiding them. Free accounts have one active reminder; extra reminders pause until Plus is active.

Which Bible translations are included?

English: King James Version or World English Bible (choose in Settings). Spanish: Reina-Valera 1909. All are in the public domain.

How do I delete everything?

Settings > Delete all data, or uninstall the app. This cannot be undone.
