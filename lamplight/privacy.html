Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 is free. Lamplight currently has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment or purchase data is processed and the purchase sections below do not apply yet. If paid features are added in a later version, this page will be updated before they launch.

Effective 13 November 2026 · Resumen en español al final

In short: Lamplight has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your prayer journal, prayer history, name and reminders are stored only on your device. We never see them.

Who we are

Lamplight is made by Waeil Mikhaeil ("we", "us"). Questions about privacy: wael.rooma@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Everything you write or do in the app is saved in a database on your phone or tablet and is not sent to us or anyone else:

  • prayer journal entries, categories, and whether and how a prayer was answered
  • which devotionals and guided prayers you finished, and on which days
  • reminder times, your optional first name, and settings such as language, Bible translation and theme

Journal entries can be very personal and may reveal your religious beliefs. That is why we built Lamplight so that this data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it.

Your device's own backup (iCloud or Google backup) may include the app's data, under the settings and policies of Apple or Google. You can turn this off in your device settings.

What is processed by others

Purchases

If you subscribe to Lamplight Plus, payment is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat, Inc. to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase receipts and basic device information (such as country and app version). It does not receive your journal or prayer history. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Crash reports (only if enabled)

Lamplight does not currently send crash reports.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. The reminder text is generic (“Time to pray”) so that nothing personal shows on your lock screen. We do not use push notification servers.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no cross-app tracking.
  • No analytics on what you read, pray or write.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

Your choices and rights

  • Export: Settings > Export journal saves your prayers as text and a spreadsheet (part of Lamplight Plus). If you need a copy of your data without Plus, email us and we will help.
  • Delete: Settings > Delete all data erases everything on the device. Uninstalling the app does the same.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel in your App Store or Google Play account.

Because we do not hold your journal or history, we cannot access, correct or delete it for you. For purchase records held by RevenueCat, email us and we will ask RevenueCat to delete them. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state privacy laws) you may have further rights, including to complain to your data protection authority.

Legal basis (EEA and UK)

We process purchase data to perform our contract with you (providing Plus). We do not process data about your religious beliefs on our servers; it stays on your device under your control.

Children

Lamplight is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
