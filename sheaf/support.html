Common questions

How do verse links work?

Type a reference the way you would write it down: "John 3:16", "Rom 8:28-30", "1 Cor 13:4-7", "Psalm 23" or "Eph 2:8-9; 4:32". Sheaf turns it into a link and lists it under "Passages in this note". Tap it to read the text and insert it into your note.

Why does a passage say its text is not built in?

Sheaf includes frequently preached verses from the King James Version. Other references still link, collect your notes and appear in the Verses tab; read their text in the Bible you use.

Where are my notes stored? Can I move them to a new phone?

Only on your device. There is no Sheaf account or cloud. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google device backup) includes them, and restoring that backup on a new phone brings them back. You can also export everything as a PDF in Settings.

How do I export or print a note?

Open the note and tap the PDF button at the top. To export every note at once, go to Settings → Export all notes as PDF.

What does Plus add?

Unlimited notebooks (two are free) and five color themes, which also color your PDFs. Notes, verse links, tags, search, reading plans and PDF export are free.

How do I cancel or restore Plus?

Cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. To restore on a new device, open Settings → Restore purchases, signed in to the same store account.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete all data erases everything on the device. Deleting the app does the same. See the privacy policy for purchase records.
