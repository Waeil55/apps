Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 is free. Sheaf currently has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment or purchase data is processed and the purchase sections below do not apply yet. If paid features are added in a later version, this page will be updated before they launch.

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Sheaf app for iPhone, iPad and Android

The short version

  • Your notes are stored only on your phone, and we never receive them. That includes titles, speakers, note text, tags, notebooks, reading plans and settings.
  • Sermon notes can reveal religious beliefs, which many laws treat as sensitive. Because your notes never leave your device unless you share them yourself, we never hold that information.
  • There is no Sheaf account. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or church.
  • There are no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking, and we never sell data.
  • If you subscribe to Plus, Apple or Google process the payment. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, sees an anonymous ID and your purchase status, and nothing about your notes.
  • You can delete everything at any time in Settings → Delete all data.

Who we are

Sheaf is made by Wael Rooma ("we", "us"). Contact: wael.rooma@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your notebooks, notes, tags, the Bible references found in them, reading plans and your progress, and your settings are stored in a local database inside the app. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. The scripture text shown in the app is bundled with the app, so looking up a verse sends nothing over the internet.

Your device's own backup system (iCloud or Google device backup) may include this data in your private backup, if you have backup turned on. That backup is controlled by your Apple or Google account, not by us.

When you export a PDF, it is created on your device and goes only where you choose to share it, for example to Files, Mail or a printer.

What is processed off the device

We do not use RevenueCat's attribution or advertising integrations, and we do not link your purchase to your identity.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

We process subscription data because it is needed to perform our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not receive the notes on your device, so we are not their controller, and we process no special-category data about you.

Children

Sheaf is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Retention and deletion

On-device data stays until you delete it. You can delete a note, a notebook or a plan, or use Settings → Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes it. RevenueCat keeps purchase records as long as needed for subscriptions, tax and accounting. To ask us to delete that record, email wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to processing. Because we hold almost nothing, most requests are handled by the delete controls in the app. For anything else, email us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Data is stored inside the app's private storage on your device and protected by your device's lock and encryption. Connections to the app stores and to RevenueCat use encryption in transit.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. If the change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
