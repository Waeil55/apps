Importing cards

  • Use two columns: the front in column 1 and the back in column 2. Extra columns are ignored.
  • Commas, semicolons and tabs all work, and Tessera detects which one your file uses. Quoted fields with commas or line breaks are fine.
  • A header row such as front,back or term,definition is skipped automatically, and you can switch this on or off in the preview.
  • Cards whose front already exists in the deck are skipped, so you can safely re-import an updated file.
  • From a spreadsheet: select the two columns, copy, then tap Paste on the import screen.

How scheduling works

After you reveal an answer, choose Again if you didn’t know it, Hard if it took effort, Good if you knew it, or Easy if it was too easy. Each button shows when you’ll see the card next. New cards go through short steps (1 and 10 minutes), then the gaps grow to days, weeks and months.

Subscriptions

  • Cancel: in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Your data
