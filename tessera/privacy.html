Privacy policy

Version 1.0 is free. Tessera currently has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment or purchase data is processed and the purchase sections below do not apply yet. If paid features are added in a later version, this page will be updated before they launch.
Short version: your decks, cards and study history stay on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no AI services and no tracking. We never see your cards.

Who we are

Tessera is published by Waeil Mikhaeil. Contact: wael.rooma@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

  • Your decks and cards, including anything you import from a CSV file or paste from the clipboard.
  • Your review schedule and study history (streak, retention and daily review counts).
  • Your settings: appearance, colour theme and new cards per day.

This data is stored locally in the app and is never uploaded to us or anyone else. Imported files are read on your device only. Deleting the app, or Settings → Erase all decks, removes it. Your device’s own backup (for example iCloud or Google backup) may include app data, depending on your device settings.

Clipboard and files

Tessera reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste on the import screen, and reads a file only when you choose one. Nothing is read in the background.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat, Inc. to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, purchase receipts and basic device information (such as the operating system and app version), never your cards or study data. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy at revenuecat.com. We never see your payment card details.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in, and no name, email, location, contacts, camera or microphone access.
  • No advertising, no advertising ID, and no selling or sharing of data.
  • No AI services: your cards are never sent anywhere to be processed.
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in this version.

Email

If you email support, we use your message and address only to answer you, and we delete them when they are no longer needed.

Children

Tessera is a general study tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Your cards and study data never leave your device, so you control them directly. For questions about purchase data held by RevenueCat, Apple or Google, or to exercise rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, contact wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date.
