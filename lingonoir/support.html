Common questions

How do I play?

Read each chapter and tap the highlighted Spanish words to see what they mean. Then examine the clue: pick a meaning, find the Spanish word, or build a sentence from tiles. The last chapter of every case asks you to spot the suspect whose statement is a lie.

What’s free?

Cases 1 and 2 in full, the notebook, and a daily interrogation of up to 10 words. Plus adds four more cases and unlimited daily interrogations.

How do stars work?

Every wrong answer or hint is a slip. No slips earns three stars, up to two slips earns two, and more earns one. You can replay a case to improve your best.

What is the daily interrogation?

A short quiz on the words in your notebook. Words you get right come back after longer and longer gaps (1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 days). Words you miss come back tomorrow.

How do streaks work?

Solve a chapter or finish an interrogation on a day to add one to your streak. Your streak stays until the end of the following day.

Which Spanish does it teach?

Neutral Spanish that works in Latin America and Spain. Where a word differs (for example billete and boleto for “ticket”), the word card tells you.

How do I restore Plus on a new phone?

Use the same Apple ID or Google account, open Settings in the app and tap “Restore purchases”.

How do I cancel Plus?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel.

I found a mistake in the Spanish.

Thank you! Email us the case, the chapter and what you expected. We fix mistakes in the next update.
