Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 is free. Cueline currently has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment or purchase data is processed and the purchase sections below do not apply yet. If paid features are added in a later version, this page will be updated before they launch.

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Cueline app for iPhone, iPad and Android

The short version

  • Your scripts are stored only on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The camera and microphone work only when you turn on the camera or record a take. Video and audio stay on your phone until you choose to share or save them.
  • There is no Cueline account, no analytics SDK, no advertising and no tracking.
  • If you subscribe, Apple or Google handles the payment. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives an anonymous id and your purchase receipt, nothing about your scripts or recordings.

Who we are

Cueline is published by Waeil Mikhaeil ("we", "us"). Contact: wael.rooma@gmail.com.

What Cueline uses on your device

DataWhyWhere it goes
Scripts you write or pasteTo show them in the library and the prompter.A small database inside the app on your device.
Settings (speed, text size, mirror, colours, appearance)To remember how you like to read.The same on-device database.

Subscriptions

Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to check whether you have an active Cueline Plus subscription. RevenueCat receives a random app user id, your store receipt, the app version and your country and currency. It does not receive scripts, settings or recordings. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Remote control

Bluetooth clickers and keyboards are paired in your phone's own settings. Cueline only reads the key presses while the prompter is open, and does not store them.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no email collection, no contacts, no location, no photo library access.
  • No advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics, no data brokers, no selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No speech recognition and no "AI" processing of your scripts or voice.

Backups

The app's database may be included in your phone's own encrypted device backup (iCloud or Google). That backup is controlled by you and your device provider.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your Cueline data: Settings → Delete my data. This removes every script and setting from the device.
  • Turn off camera or microphone access: in your phone's Settings, at any time. The prompter keeps working without them.
  • Uninstall: removes everything Cueline stored.

Because we hold no personal data about you on our servers, access or deletion requests to us usually have nothing to return. For subscription data held by Apple, Google or RevenueCat, we will help you contact them. Residents of the EEA, UK and California have rights of access, deletion and objection; write to wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Children

Cueline is a general tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app before they apply.
