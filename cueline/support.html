Common questions

How is the speed set?

In words per minute. Most people speak at 130 to 160. Cueline measures how tall your script is on screen and scrolls so you read at that pace, whatever the text size.

What are the words in [square brackets]?

Cues for yourself, like [smile] or [show product]. They appear in colour and are not counted as spoken words.

How do I use a glass teleprompter rig?

Turn on mirror mode (the arrows button in the prompter). The text flips so it reads correctly in the glass.

Where do my recordings go?

After you stop recording, tap "Share or save" and choose Save Video (iPhone) or your gallery app (Android). Takes you don't save are removed later by the system.

Which remote controls work?

Most Bluetooth presentation clickers, page turners and keyboards. Pair them in your phone's Bluetooth settings. Space, Enter or B play and pause, Page Down or the arrow keys change speed, + and − change the size. Remote control is part of Plus.

The camera or microphone doesn't work

Check Settings → Cueline on iPhone, or Settings → Apps → Cueline → Permissions on Android, and allow Camera and Microphone.

What does Plus add?

Unlimited scripts (free keeps 3), remote control and extra prompter colours. The prompter, mirror mode and recording are always free.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play under their policies.

Restore my purchase on a new phone

Open Cueline → Settings → Plan → Restore, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.
