Common questions

How do I play?

Tap any face-up card that is one rank above or below the lantern card (Kings and Aces wrap). Cards played in a row build a chain that multiplies your points, and drawing from the stock breaks it. Beat each harbor's tide target to sail on. In port, spend coins on charms, tricks and gilded ranks. Every third harbor is a storm that twists the rules. The full guide is in the app under Settings → How to play.

Can I take a move back?

Yes. Each hand gives you a few undos (shown on the undo button). Undo restores the exact previous state, including any trick you used. Gale storms and higher Currents give fewer undos.

What is the Daily Crossing?

The Daily Crossing is a new run each day, and it's the same run for everyone who plays that day. Your first finished attempt each day counts toward your streak. The Daily Crossing is free forever.

I bought Cardwake Premium on another device. How do I get it back?

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account. Then go to Settings → Purchases → Restore purchase, or tap Restore on the upgrade screen. A purchase made through Apple doesn't carry over to Google, or the other way round.

How do I get a refund?

Apple and Google handle all refunds. For Apple, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google, go to play.google.com, open Order history and request a refund.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Your progress is stored only on your device, because there is no account. A full device backup and restore through iCloud or Google usually brings it across. You can always restore the purchase, even if your progress doesn't come across.

Accessibility options

Settings has Reduce motion (it also follows your system setting) and switches for sound and haptics. Text follows your system text size. Every card is labelled for VoiceOver and TalkBack with its rank, suit and the points it would score, and suits always show a shape as well as a colour.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Reset progress erases everything Cardwake has stored on your device, and deleting the app also removes it. We keep no gameplay data on any server. See the Privacy Policy.
