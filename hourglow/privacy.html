Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Hourglow app for iPhone, iPad and Android

The short version

  • Your fasts, water logs, plans and settings are stored only on your phone, and we never receive them.
  • There is no Hourglow account. We never ask for your name, email, weight, age or any other health detail. During setup you only tap to confirm you are 18 or older, and that answer stays on your phone.
  • Hourglow does not read or write Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • There are no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking, and we never sell data.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. No push server is involved.
  • If you subscribe to Plus, Apple or Google process the payment. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, sees an anonymous ID and your purchase status, and nothing about your fasting.
  • You can delete everything at any time in Settings → Delete all data.

Who we are

Hourglow is made by Wael Rooma ("we", "us"). Contact: wael.rooma@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

The start and end times of your fasts, your chosen plans, the water you log each day, your reminder times and your settings are stored in a local database inside the app. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Your device's own backup (iCloud or Google device backup) may include this data in your private backup if you have backup turned on. That backup is controlled by your Apple or Google account, not by us.

What is processed off the device

WhoWhatWhy
Apple App Store or Google PlayYour purchase and payment, under their own privacy policiesTo sell and renew a Plus subscription
RevenueCat, Inc. (our processor)An anonymous app user ID, the products bought, purchase and renewal dates, store receipt data, and your country and app versionTo check whether Plus is active and to restore purchases

We do not use RevenueCat's attribution or advertising integrations, and we do not link your purchase to your identity.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Hourglow asks your phone for permission and schedules the reminders locally. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings at any time.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

We process subscription data because it is needed to perform our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not receive the fasting and water data on your device, so we are not its controller.

Children

Hourglow is for adults only and is not directed at children. The app asks people to confirm they are 18 or older before they can use it.

Retention and deletion

On-device data stays until you delete it. You can delete a single fast, or use Settings → Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes it. RevenueCat keeps purchase records as long as needed for subscriptions, tax and accounting. To ask us to delete that record, email wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA and Washington's My Health My Data Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to processing. Because we hold almost nothing, most requests are handled by the delete controls in the app. For anything else, email us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Data is stored inside the app's private storage on your device and protected by your device's lock and encryption. Connections to the app stores and to RevenueCat use encryption in transit.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. If the change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
