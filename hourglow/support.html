Common questions

How do I start and end a fast?

On Today, tap Start fast when you finish your last meal. The ring fills as you go and shows when you reach your goal. Tap End fast when you eat again. If you end before your goal, Hourglow asks first, and you can tap Undo in the message that appears.

I forgot to tap Start. Can I change the start time?

Yes. While a fast is running, tap the Started tile on Today and move the time back by 15 minutes or an hour at a time. For a finished fast, open History and tap it to change its start or end, or to delete it.

Which plan should I choose?

That is a choice for you and, if you have any health questions, your doctor. Hourglow offers 16:8 (16 hours fasting, 8-hour eating window), 18:6 and OMAD (one meal a day). With Plus you can also make your own plan from 12 to 24 hours.

How does the streak work?

A day counts when a fast that reached its goal ends on that day. Your streak is the run of those days up to today. It keeps counting through yesterday while today's fast is still running, so it never looks broken mid-fast.

Why didn't I get a reminder?

Check that the reminder is on in Hourglow's Settings and that notifications are allowed for Hourglow in your phone's settings. Focus modes and battery savers can also hold notifications back.

Does Hourglow work with Apple Health or Health Connect?

Not in this version. Your data stays inside Hourglow on your phone.

How do I restore Plus on a new phone?

Open Settings → See Plus → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account. Your history moves only through your phone's own backup or transfer.

How do I cancel Plus?

On iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android, go to Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Your history stays, and the free features keep working.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete all data.

Hourglow is a timer and journal, not medical advice. For questions about your health, diet or medication, please speak to a doctor.
