Common questions

When does a new puzzle arrive?

At midnight in your local time zone. Monday's grids are the gentlest and Sunday's are the hardest.

Does every puzzle have one answer?

Yes. A solver checks each grid before you see it, so it can be solved by logic alone, without guessing.

How do streaks work?

Solve any daily puzzle on its own day to keep your streak. Your streak stays safe until midnight, so there is still time to solve today's grid.

What do hints do?

You get three per grid. A hint first fixes a mistake if you have one, and otherwise reveals one piece of the answer.

I bought Plus on another device

Open You, then Lattice Plus, then Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account.

How do I cancel Plus?

iPhone and iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Android: Google Play, Profile, Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions.

My progress disappeared

Progress is stored only on your device, so deleting the app or clearing its data removes it. There is no cloud backup in this version.
