Common questions

How do I start my wind-down?

On Tonight, tap “Start wind-down”. The player shows one step at a time with a soft timer. Tap “Next step” when you are ready; there is no rush.

How do I change or edit my routine?

On Tonight, tap your routine, then pick another one or tap the pencil to edit its steps. Tap a step's minutes to change its length.

How do I log a night?

In the morning, tap a face on the “How did you sleep?” card, or open Diary → “Log last night”. Adjust the times if they differ from your schedule and tap Save.

The sound stops when my phone locks

Make sure the phone isn't in Low Power Mode and the app's volume is up. Sounds keep playing in the background until the sleep timer ends; set the timer to “Off” to play until you stop it.

I’m not getting reminders

Check Settings → Reminders in Hushhour, then make sure notifications for Hushhour are allowed in your phone’s settings. Focus modes and battery savers can also delay notifications.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, then go to Settings → Restore purchases. Your diary itself stays on the old device unless you exported it or your device backup includes it.

How do I cancel Plus?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Hushhour. On Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Hushhour. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Your data → Delete all data. We hold no copy of your diary, so this deletes it completely.

Hushhour is a wellness tool, not a medical device. If poor sleep continues, please talk to a doctor.
