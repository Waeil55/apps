Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Hushhour app for iPhone, iPad and Android

The short version

  • Your sleep diary, wind-down routines and settings is stored only on your device. We never receive it.
  • There is no Hushhour account. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No ads, no advertising IDs, no tracking across apps or websites, and no analytics on how you sleep.
  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • You can export or delete everything from Settings at any time.

Who we are

Hushhour is provided by Wael Rooma ("we", "us"). Contact us at wael.rooma@gmail.com with any privacy question.

Data that stays on your device

Hushhour works without a server. What you create is stored in a private database inside the app:

  • Diary entries: the night, bed and wake times, time to fall asleep, times woken, how rested you felt, tags you pick (such as "late caffeine") and an optional note
  • Wind-down routines: title, icon, steps and step lengths, plus which nights you finished a wind-down
  • Settings, such as your bedtime, wake time, theme, sound and reminder preferences

Sounds are generated on your device; the app never uses your microphone. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device and don't pass through any server.

Device backups. If iCloud Backup or Google device backup is on, your device may include the app's data in that backup. That backup is controlled by Apple or Google under your account, not by us.

Exports. If you export your data, the file goes only where you choose to send or save it.

Data processed to manage your subscription

If you start a trial or subscribe to Hushhour Plus, payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card details.

To check whether your subscription is active and to restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. as our service provider. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created on your device (not your name or email)
  • your purchase and subscription records from Apple or Google (product, dates, status, country, price)
  • basic technical data such as app version, operating system version and IP address

RevenueCat never receives your diary. It processes this data only on our behalf, under its own privacy policy.

Crash reports

This version of Hushhour does not send crash reports. If we turn crash reporting on, we will update this policy first, and reports will contain only technical diagnostics, never your diary.

What we don't do

  • We don't use advertising SDKs, advertising IDs or tracking pixels.
  • We don't sell, rent or share personal data, including for targeted advertising.
  • We don't use your data to train AI models.

Legal bases (UK and EU users)

Your diary is processed on your device to provide the app you asked for. Subscription data is processed to perform our contract with you (providing Hushhour Plus) and to meet legal obligations such as tax records.

Your rights and choices

  • Access and portability: Settings → Your data → Export my data.
  • Deletion: Settings → Your data → Delete everything erases all data in the app. Deleting the app also removes it.
  • Subscription data: email us to request access to or deletion of the records held by RevenueCat. Some purchase records may need to be kept where the law requires.
  • Complaints: if you are in the UK or EU, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Retention

Data on your device stays until you delete it. Subscription records are kept by RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal and tax obligations.

Children

Hushhour is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly process data about children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.

Hushhour is a wellness and routine tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any sleep condition. Your diary is not shared with any doctor, insurer or employer.
