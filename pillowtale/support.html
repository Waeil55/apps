Common questions

What is tonight's story?

Every night at midnight, local time, Pillowtale picks a different story from the whole library and makes it free for everyone. It works through every story before one repeats.

What is lamp mode?

Lamp mode shows the story in warm amber text on black, which is gentler in a dark bedroom. Tap the lamp button while reading, or turn it on for every story in For grown-ups.

Why does the app ask a times-table question?

That is the parental gate. Pillowtale is made for children, so purchases, links that leave the app, and settings are kept behind a question an adult can answer easily.

Can the story say my child's name?

Yes. Add a first name in For grown-ups and every story ends with a goodnight for them. The name stays on your device.

I bought Plus on another device

Open For grown-ups, then Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account.

How do I cancel Plus?

iPhone and iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Android: Google Play, Profile, Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions.

My favorites disappeared

Favorites and reading progress are stored only on your device, so deleting the app or clearing its data removes them. There is no cloud backup in this version.
