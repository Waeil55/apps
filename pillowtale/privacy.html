Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 is free. Pillowtale currently has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment or purchase data is processed and the purchase sections below do not apply yet. If paid features are added in a later version, this page will be updated before they launch.

Effective 13 November 2026 · Wael Rooma

  • Pillowtale collects no personal information from children or parents.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising identifier.
  • Favorites, reading progress, settings and the optional first name stay on your device.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google, with RevenueCat checking that a purchase is valid.

Made for children, run by parents

Pillowtale is a library of original bedtime stories that a parent or caregiver reads aloud from the screen. It is designed for families with young children and follows the App Store Kids category rules and the Google Play Families policy. Purchases, links that leave the app, and settings sit behind a parental gate: a question an adult answers before continuing.

What stays on your device

The stories are built into the app. Your favorites, the page you reached in each story, how many times a story was finished, and your text size, lamp mode and appearance settings are stored only in the app's local storage. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

The optional first name

A parent can type a child's first name so that each story ends with a personal goodnight. The name is stored only on the device, is never sent to us or anyone else, and can be removed at any time in For grown-ups. Leave it blank if you prefer.

Purchases

If a parent subscribes to Pillowtale Plus, the App Store or Google Play processes the payment. We never see card details. The app uses RevenueCat, a service provider, to confirm that the subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID and the purchase history from the store. It does not receive a name, an email address, the child's first name or any reading data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads of any kind, and we do not use an advertising identifier.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not offer chat, sharing, social features or user accounts.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.

Children's privacy (COPPA and GDPR-K)

We do not knowingly collect, use or disclose personal information from children. Because nothing leaves the device except the anonymous purchase check described above, there is no child data for us to hold, review or delete. Parents with questions can write to wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you or your child, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can delete all app data at any time by deleting the app. Manage or cancel a subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, explain them in the app's release notes.
