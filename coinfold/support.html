Common questions

How does envelope budgeting work in Coinfold?

Money you record as income goes to "Ready to assign". Open an envelope and type how much of it goes there this month. When Ready to assign reaches zero, all your money has a job. Each expense you log comes out of the envelope you pick.

What happens at the end of the month?

Money left in an envelope rolls into next month automatically. If an envelope is overspent, it starts the next month at zero and the shortfall is taken from next month's Ready to assign, once, so you can see it and plan for it. In a new month, "Assign like last month" copies your previous amounts in one tap.

Can Coinfold connect to my bank?

No, by design. You type each amount, which keeps your bank login private and helps you notice what you spend.

I made a mistake in an entry. How do I fix it?

Open Activity and tap the entry. Change the amount, envelope, date or note and tap Save, or delete it. After deleting, tap Undo in the message that appears if you change your mind.

What happens if I delete an envelope?

The money assigned to it goes back to Ready to assign. Its past expenses stay in Activity as "Uncategorised", so your history still adds up.

Does changing the currency convert my amounts?

No. It only changes how amounts are shown. Pick the currency you budget in.

How do I restore Plus on a new phone?

Open Settings → See Plus → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account. Your budget moves only through your phone's own backup or transfer.

How do I cancel Plus?

On iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android, go to Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Your budget stays, and the free features keep working.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete all data.
