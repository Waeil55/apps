Common questions

How do I play?

Tap a patch of soil to plant a seed from your pouch or to water it. Each plant likes a certain amount of light and moisture, and plants change the patches around them: clover enriches the soil, trees cast shade, and moss keeps the ground damp. Flowers in bloom bring bees, and bees help plants set seed. Mature plants fill your pouch and sometimes sow themselves nearby. The journal suggests gentle goals that unlock new species.

Does my garden grow when the app is closed?

Yes. Grove simulates up to 24 hours of growth while you are away, using your device clock. After a longer break your garden simply rests and picks up again when you return. Nothing dies suddenly: a plant in the wrong spot slowly fades and returns to the soil, which feeds the patch.

Are there timers, energy or ads?

No. There are no ads, no energy bars, no notifications nagging you to come back, and nothing to buy except the optional one-time Grove Premium.

I bought Grove Premium on another device. How do I get it back?

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account. Then go to Settings → Purchases → Restore purchase, or tap Restore on the upgrade screen. A purchase made through Apple doesn't carry over to Google, or the other way round.

How do I get a refund?

Apple and Google handle all refunds. For Apple, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google, go to play.google.com, open Order history and request a refund.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Your gardens are stored only on your device, because there is no account. A full device backup and restore through iCloud or Google usually brings it across. You can always restore the purchase, even if your gardens don't come across.

Accessibility options

Animations (bees and rain) follow your system Reduce Motion setting. Settings has a haptics switch and light, dark or system appearance. Text follows your system text size. Every patch is labelled for VoiceOver and TalkBack with the plant, its stage and its position, and the inspector describes light, moisture and nutrients in words as well as bars.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → "Start this grove again" clears the current garden, and deleting the app erases everything Grove has stored on your device. We keep no gameplay data on any server. See the Privacy Policy.
