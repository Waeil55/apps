Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Sprigwell app for iPhone, iPad and Android

The short version

  • Your plants, schedules, care notes, care history and photos are stored only on your phone. We never receive them.
  • There is no Sprigwell account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself. No push server is involved.
  • The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to add a plant photo.
  • There are no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking, and we never sell data.
  • If you subscribe to Plus, Apple or Google process the payment. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, sees an anonymous ID and your purchase status, and nothing about your plants.
  • You can delete everything at any time in Settings → Delete all data.

Who we are

Sprigwell is made by Wael Rooma ("we", "us"). Contact: wael.rooma@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your plants and their names, species, locations, light, watering and feeding schedules, care history, care notes, photo log and settings are stored in a local database and a private folder inside the app. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Your device's own backup system (iCloud or Google device backup) may include this data in your private backup, if you have backup turned on. That backup is controlled by your Apple or Google account, not by us.

Camera, photos and notifications

  • Camera: used only when you tap "Take photo". The photo is saved inside Sprigwell.
  • Photo library: Sprigwell uses the system photo picker, so it sees only the photo you pick, and copies it into the app.
  • Notifications: if you turn on reminders, your phone schedules them locally. They contain plant names and are never sent through a server.

You can change these permissions at any time in your phone's settings.

What is processed off the device

We do not use RevenueCat's attribution or advertising integrations, and we do not link your purchase to your identity.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

We process subscription data because it is needed to perform our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not receive the plant data on your device, so we are not its controller.

Children

Sprigwell is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Retention and deletion

On-device data stays until you delete it. You can delete a photo, delete a plant (with its history and photos), or use Settings → Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes it. RevenueCat keeps purchase records as long as needed for subscriptions, tax and accounting. To ask us to delete that record, email wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to processing. Because we hold almost nothing, most requests are handled by the delete controls in the app. For anything else, email us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Data is stored inside the app's private storage on your device and protected by your device's lock and encryption. Connections to the app stores and to RevenueCat use encryption in transit.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. If the change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
