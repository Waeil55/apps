Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 is free. Paperkeep currently has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment or purchase data is processed and the purchase sections below do not apply yet. If paid features are added in a later version, this page will be updated before they launch.

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Paperkeep app for iPhone, iPad and Android.

The short version

  • Your documents, page images, titles, tags, notes, folders and expiry dates are stored only on your device. We never receive them.
  • There is no account, no cloud sync, no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDK.
  • The only network service is the app store purchase system (through RevenueCat) if you subscribe to Paperkeep Plus.
  • Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Delete all data, erases everything.

Who we are

Paperkeep is published by Waeil Mikhaeil ("we", "us"). Contact: wael.rooma@gmail.com.

What the app stores, and where

DataWhere it livesWhy
Page images you scan or importThe app's private folder on your deviceTo show and export your documents
Titles, tags, notes, folders, expiry datesA database on your deviceTo organise, search and remind
Passcode (if you turn on the lock)Only a salted one-way hash, on your deviceTo check the passcode; the passcode itself is never stored
Settings (theme, reminder timing)On your deviceTo remember your choices

We do not operate a server for Paperkeep and cannot see, recover or restore any of this data. Your device's own backups (iCloud or Google backup) may include the app's data, according to your device settings and those providers' policies.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only while you are scanning. On iPhone the system document scanner (VisionKit) and on Android Google's ML Kit document scanner process the images on your device. If you import photos, only the photos you choose are copied into the app. We never read the rest of your photo library.

Reminders

Expiry reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are not sent through a push server. A reminder shows the title you gave the document.

Sharing a PDF

When you choose Share as PDF, the app creates the PDF on your device and opens your system share sheet. Whatever you then share goes to the app or person you pick, under their terms. We are not involved.

Purchases

If you subscribe to Paperkeep Plus, payment is handled by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase receipts and basic device information (such as platform and app version). It never receives your documents or anything you type into the app. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, ad IDs or data brokers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs in this version.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No text recognition sent to any server.

Security

Documents sit in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read, and are covered by your device's encryption when it is locked with a passcode. The optional Paperkeep passcode lock stops people who pick up your unlocked phone from opening the vault; it is an app lock, not separate file encryption. Keep your device updated and protected with a strong device passcode.

Your choices and rights

Because your data stays on your device, you control it directly: edit or delete any document, use Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the app. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California law) you have rights to access, correct, delete and port personal data and to complain to a regulator. We hold no personal data about you beyond what Apple, Google and RevenueCat process for purchases; contact us and we will help with any request.

Children

Paperkeep is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect children's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
