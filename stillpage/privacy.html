Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 is free. Stillpage currently has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment or purchase data is processed and the purchase sections below do not apply yet. If paid features are added in a later version, this page will be updated before they launch.

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Stillpage app for iPhone, iPad and Android

The short version

  • Your entries, moods, feeling tags and settings are stored only on your phone. We never receive them.
  • There is no Stillpage account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • There is no AI. Nobody, human or machine, reads your journal.
  • There are no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking, and we never sell data.
  • If you subscribe to Plus, Apple or Google process the payment. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, sees an anonymous ID and your purchase status, and nothing you write.
  • You can delete everything at any time in Settings → Delete everything.

Who we are

Stillpage is made by Waeil Mikhaeil ("we", "us"). Contact: wael.rooma@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Journal entries, the prompt each entry answered, moods, feeling tags, your draft, reminder time, theme and passcode settings are stored in a local database inside the app. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Your device's own backup system (iCloud or Google device backup) may include this data in your private backup if you have backup turned on. That backup is controlled by your Apple or Google account, not by us.

If you set a passcode, the app stores only a salted, one-way hash of it, never the passcode itself. The passcode hides the app from people using your phone. It does not add extra encryption to the database beyond the protection your phone already provides.

Exports (Markdown, PDF or JSON) are created on your device and go only where you choose to send them.

Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push server is involved.

What is processed off the device

Children

Stillpage is a general-audience journal and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, of any age: what you write stays on your device.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

We process subscription data because it is needed to perform our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not receive the journal data on your device, so we are not its controller.

Retention and deletion

On-device data stays until you delete it. You can delete a single entry, or erase everything with Settings → Delete everything. Deleting the app also removes it. RevenueCat keeps purchase records as long as needed for subscriptions, tax and accounting. To ask us to delete that record, email wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to processing. Because we hold almost nothing, most requests are handled by the controls in the app. For anything else, email us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Data is stored inside the app's private storage on your device and protected by your device's lock and encryption. Connections to the app stores and to RevenueCat use encryption in transit.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. If the change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
