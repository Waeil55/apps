Common questions

Where are my entries stored?

Only on your phone, inside the app. There is no account and no cloud. Your phone's own iCloud or Google backup may include them if backup is on.

I forgot my passcode. Can you reset it?

No. We never receive your passcode or your entries, so there is nothing we can reset. On the lock screen, "Forgot passcode?" lets you erase the journal on this device and start fresh. Export regularly with Plus if you want a copy.

How do streaks work?

A streak counts calendar days with at least one entry. It stays alive until the end of the day after your last entry, so it is never broken before your day is over.

Does Stillpage use AI?

No. Every prompt is written by people and ships with the app. Nothing you write is analysed.

How do I move my journal to a new phone?

Restore the new phone from a device backup, or export a JSON backup and Markdown copy with Plus before you switch.

How do I cancel Plus?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions. Your entries stay when Plus ends.

Restore purchases

Open Settings → Stillpage Plus → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to subscribe.

If you are struggling

Stillpage is a journal, not a mental health service. If you are going through a hard time, please reach out to someone you trust or a qualified professional. If you feel unsafe, contact your local emergency number now.
