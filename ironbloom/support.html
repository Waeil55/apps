Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone, inside the app. There's no account and we never receive your logs. See the Privacy Policy.

Will Ironbloom tell me if I'm in perimenopause?

No. Ironbloom doesn't diagnose, score or label anything. It keeps a clear record of what you logged so you can talk it through with a clinician, who can help interpret it.

Why doesn't Ironbloom predict my period?

Cycles often become irregular around menopause, and a prediction could mislead you. Ironbloom shows the days you logged bleeding and the gaps between them, in your own dates. It also prompts you to contact a doctor about bleeding after 12 months without a period, or very heavy bleeding.

Why is my medication shown exactly as I typed it?

Ironbloom records your medication as written by your prescriber or on the label. It never calculates, converts or suggests a dose or treatment. If something changes, update it in Settings → Medications and reminders.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Your phone's own backup (iCloud Backup or Google backup) usually restores app data when you set up a new phone. You can also use Settings → Your data → Export all data (JSON) or Export spreadsheets (CSV) to keep a copy.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Your data → Delete all data. This can't be undone, so export first if you want a copy.

My reminders aren't arriving

Check that notifications are allowed for Ironbloom in your phone's settings, that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing them, and (on Android) that battery optimisation isn't stopping the app. Then open Settings → Reminders in Ironbloom and save again.

How do I restore my Ironbloom Plus purchase?

Open Settings → Subscription → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Ironbloom. On Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Ironbloom. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple or Google, so refunds are handled by them: reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, or the Google Play order history for Android.

Does Ironbloom connect to Apple Health or Health Connect?

Not yet. It's planned for a future version.
