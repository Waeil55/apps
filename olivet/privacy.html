Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · Wael Rooma

  • Olivet collects no personal information from children or from parents.
  • No ads, no analytics, no accounts and no tracking.
  • Reading progress and settings stay on your device.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google, with RevenueCat checking that a purchase is valid.

What stays on your device

Olivet stores which stories you have read, the page you stopped on, the reading length you chose (Little or Big listeners), the text size and the appearance setting. This is kept only in the app's local storage on your device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it, and Grown-ups → "Clear reading progress" clears it inside the app.

Children's privacy

Olivet is designed to be read by a parent or caregiver with a young child. It does not ask for or collect a name, photo, birthday, email, location, contacts, voice or any other personal information, from a child or from anyone else. There is no chat, no sharing and no user-generated content. We follow the principles of the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR's rules for children's data by not collecting personal data at all.

Purchases

If you buy All Stories, the App Store or Google Play processes your payment. We never see your card details. Purchases can only be started by a grown-up, after a parental check. The app uses RevenueCat, a service provider, to confirm that your purchase is valid. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID and the purchase record from the store. It does not receive a name, email or reading data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads or use an advertising identifier.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools in this version.
  • We do not sell, share or rent personal data, because we do not have any.
  • We do not send push notifications or emails.

Links

The only links out of the app (this policy, the terms and support email) are in the Grown-ups area, behind a parental check.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you or your child, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can remove all app data at any time by deleting the app. Questions: wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, explain them in the app's release notes.
