The app

Olivet is a collection of children's Bible stories for reading aloud, provided by Wael Rooma. By using it you agree to these terms and to the standard licence terms of the store you downloaded it from (Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement or Google Play's Terms of Service).

The stories

The stories are original retellings written for Olivet. They are simplified for young children and are not a translation of the Bible. Each story shows where to find the full account in the Bible. Memory verses are quoted from the King James Version.

What is free

Six stories are free, with no time limit: God Makes Everything, Noah and the Big Boat, David and the Giant, Jesus Is Born, Jesus Calms the Storm and The Lost Sheep.
