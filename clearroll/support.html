Common questions

Does Clearroll upload my photos?

No. Everything happens on your phone. There is no account and no server that receives your library.

How does it decide something is a duplicate?

Two items are duplicates only if they have the same type, dimensions and file size and identical contents (checked with a SHA-256 fingerprint on your phone). "Similar shots" are photos taken seconds apart, like bursts and retakes. You always choose which to keep.

I deleted something by mistake

On iPhone and iPad, open Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted. Items stay there for 30 days. On Android, check your gallery's Trash or Bin; some devices delete straight away.

Why is the space freed different from what Settings shows?

Clearroll adds up the file sizes your phone reports. Storage figures in Settings can lag, and iOS keeps deleted items in Recently Deleted until they are emptied. If you use iCloud Photos with "Optimise Storage", the phone may hold smaller versions than the full files.

Clearroll only sees some of my photos

You probably allowed access to selected photos only. In the app, tap "Choose more" on the home screen, or change it in your phone's Settings → Clearroll → Photos.

What does Plus add?

Unlimited deleting (free covers a daily allowance), smart grouping such as keeping the suggested shot in every group with one tap, and a monthly reminder. Scanning and reviewing are always free.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play under their policies.

Restore my purchase on a new phone

Open Clearroll → Settings → Plan → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.
