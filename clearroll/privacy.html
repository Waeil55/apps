Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 is free. Clearroll currently has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment or purchase data is processed and the purchase sections below do not apply yet. If paid features are added in a later version, this page will be updated before they launch.

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Clearroll app for iPhone, iPad and Android

The short version

  • Clearroll looks at your photo library only on your phone. Your photos, videos, file names, dates and locations are never uploaded. We never receive them.
  • There is no Clearroll account, no analytics SDK, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The only things Clearroll saves are your settings and a running total of how many items and how much space you have freed. No file names, ids or pictures are stored.
  • If you subscribe, Apple or Google handles the payment. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives an anonymous id and your purchase receipt, nothing about your photos.

Who we are

Clearroll is published by Waeil Mikhaeil ("we", "us"). Contact: wael.rooma@gmail.com.

What Clearroll accesses on your device

DataWhyWhere it goes
Photo and video library (with your permission)To find exact duplicates, similar shots, screenshots and large videos, and to delete the items you confirm.Stays on your device. Never uploaded.
File contents of possible duplicatesTo calculate a fingerprint (SHA-256) that proves two files are identical. Only files with the same size and dimensions are checked.Calculated in memory on your device and discarded after the scan.

Clearroll never downloads iCloud originals just to measure them, and it does not read photo locations.

Deleting photos

Clearroll deletes only the items you have marked and confirmed on the review screen, and your phone's system dialog asks you to confirm again. On iPhone and iPad, deleted items move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days. On Android, some devices delete immediately; the app tells you this before you confirm.

Subscriptions

Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to check whether you have an active Clearroll Plus subscription. RevenueCat receives a random app user id, your store receipt, the app version and your country and currency. It does not receive photos, file names or anything from your library. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no email collection, no contacts, no location, no camera or microphone.
  • No advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics, no data brokers, no selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No uploading of photos for "AI" processing. All matching is done by deterministic code on your device.

Backups

The app's small settings database may be included in your phone's own encrypted device backup (iCloud or Google). It contains only settings and totals, never details of your photos.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your Clearroll data: Settings → Delete Clearroll data. This clears history and settings. Your photo library is not affected.
  • Turn off photo access: in your phone's Settings, at any time.
  • Uninstall: removes everything Clearroll stored.

Because we hold no personal data about you on our servers, access or deletion requests to us usually have nothing to return. For subscription data held by Apple, Google or RevenueCat, we will help you contact them. Residents of the EEA, UK and California have rights of access, deletion and objection; write to wael.rooma@gmail.com.

Children

Clearroll is a general utility for adults and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app before they apply.
