Common questions

How do I play?

Read each story page and tap Continue. Each pane of a window is a puzzle. Lightpath: tap tiles to turn them until the lantern lights every pane. Tint: tapping a pane shifts it and its four neighbours one colour along; match the maker's card. Rings: pick a ring, then turn it until the window at the top spells the answer to the clue. Some rings turn others with them; the line under the buttons tells you which.

I'm stuck. Is there a hint?

Yes. Tap Hint under any puzzle as often as you like. Hints are always free. Undo takes back your last move, and Start over gives you a fresh board.

Is there anything to buy?

No. Fableglass 1.0 is free. Every chapter is included, and there is nothing to buy or restore.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Your progress is stored only on your device, because there is no account. A full device backup and restore through iCloud or Google usually brings it across.

Accessibility options

Settings has Reduce motion (it also follows your system setting) and a switch for haptics. Text follows your system text size. Every tile, pane and ring is labelled for VoiceOver and TalkBack, and each tint also shows a shape (none, a dot or a ring), so no puzzle depends on colour alone.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Start the story over erases everything Fableglass has stored on your device, and deleting the app also removes it. We keep no gameplay data on any server. See the Privacy Policy.
